## Reading rules

- “Conditionally supported” is limited to the exact assumptions and evidence stated in the row.
- A passing algebra script does not validate missing diagram multiplicities, counterterms, EFT subtraction, or a physical observable.
- Historical artifacts naming earlier source versions are provenance, not evidence that the same workflow ran against the frozen v153 baseline.
- The B1/B3 algebraic Wick-sign choice is resolved under the declared real-scalar trace and denominator convention. The complex external projection shows that B1/B3 are absent from the fixed unbroken (H,S,H^dagger,S^dagger) amplitude. For the fixed complex chirality flow, the exact finite-mixing 16-path sum is one interaction-basis propagator trace; it is not 16 independent diagrams. The unbroken limit selects path 0101. The VEV-suppressed hard/soft corrections and complete model-level Wilson vector remain open. Do not transfer signs or coefficients between these different matching objects without an explicit projection.
- CL-005 remains unresolved until all relevant matching sectors and the specified observable-level calculation are closed.
