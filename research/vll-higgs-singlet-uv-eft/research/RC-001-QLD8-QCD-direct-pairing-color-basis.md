# RC-001 QLD8: direct-pairing color basis for the \(d^\dagger d\) QCD pilot

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Stage:** P1.3a — color basis aligned with the un-Fierzed source pairing  
**Status:** all six direct-basis exchange matrices, the basis transformation to the crossed representation, and color conservation pass exact SU(3) checks.

## 1. Why a direct-pairing basis is useful

The source operator is naturally written as
\[
\mathcal O_{d^\dagger d}^{(8)}
=(D_\mu J_d^\dagger)(D^\mu J_d),\qquad
J_d=\bar d_RY_d^\dagger Q_L,
\]
whose fermion factors are
\[
(\bar Q_iP_Rd_i)(\bar d_jP_LQ_j),
\]
with direct spinor pairings \((\bar Q,d)\) and \((\bar d,Q)\). The QCD one-loop open-line numerators can be kept in this pairing; there is no need to Fierz the spinors into \((\bar Q,Q)(\bar d,d)\) before renormalization.

Use the external color-index order \((i,j,k,l)=(\bar Q,Q,\bar d,d)\). Define
\[
C_{1,d}^{ij;kl}=\delta_{il}\delta_{kj},\qquad
C_{8,d}^{ij;kl}=T^A_{il}T^A_{kj}.
\]
The tree source is simply
\[
c_d^{\rm tree}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Both singlet and octet structures must be retained after QCD exchange.

## 2. Relation to the crossed-current color basis

The crossed-current basis used in the earlier P1.1a note is
\[
C_{1,x}^{ij;kl}=\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl},\qquad
C_{8,x}^{ij;kl}=T^A_{ij}T^A_{kl}.
\]
SU(3) completeness gives
\[
C_{1,d}=\frac13C_{1,x}+2C_{8,x},
\]
\[
C_{8,d}=\frac49C_{1,x}-\frac13C_{8,x}.
\]
Thus
\[
\begin{pmatrix}C_{1,d}&C_{8,d}\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}C_{1,x}&C_{8,x}\end{pmatrix}
R,\qquad
R=
\begin{pmatrix}
1/3&4/9\\
2&-1/3
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The tree vector \((1,0)_d\) is therefore \((1/3,2)_x\), as in P1.1a.

If \(M_x\) is the color-exchange matrix in the crossed basis, the direct-basis matrix is
\[
M_d=R^{-1}M_xR.
\]
The exact script also computes the direct matrices independently from the Gell-Mann generators and verifies this similarity relation.

## 3. Six pairwise QCD exchange matrices in the direct basis

With all external legs outgoing for color-charge bookkeeping,
\[
T_q^A=T^A,\qquad T_{\bar q}^A=-T^{A\,T},
\qquad \operatorname{tr}(T^AT^B)=\frac12\delta^{AB},
\]
and
\[
\mathbf T_a\cdot\mathbf T_b=\sum_A T_a^AT_b^A,
\]
the matrices in \((C_{1,d},C_{8,d})\) are:

| Exchanged legs | \(M_{ab}^{(d)}\) |
|---|---|
| \((\bar Q,Q)\) | \(\begin{pmatrix}0&-2/9\\-1&-7/6\end{pmatrix}\) |
| \((\bar Q,\bar d)\) | \(\begin{pmatrix}0&2/9\\1&-1/3\end{pmatrix}\) |
| \((\bar Q,d)\) | \(\begin{pmatrix}-4/3&0\\0&1/6\end{pmatrix}\) |
| \((Q,\bar d)\) | \(\begin{pmatrix}-4/3&0\\0&1/6\end{pmatrix}\) |
| \((Q,d)\) | \(\begin{pmatrix}0&2/9\\1&-1/3\end{pmatrix}\) |
| \((\bar d,d)\) | \(\begin{pmatrix}0&-2/9\\-1&-7/6\end{pmatrix}\) |

The two within-source exchanges \((\bar Q,d)\) and \((Q,\bar d)\) are diagonal in this basis. The four cross-source exchanges mix the direct singlet and octet structures.

Acting on the tree source vector \((1,0)_d\), the six color actions are
particularly simple:
\[
\begin{aligned}
M_{\bar Q,d}^{(d)}(1,0)^T
&=M_{Q,\bar d}^{(d)}(1,0)^T
=-C_F(1,0)^T,\\
M_{\bar Q,Q}^{(d)}(1,0)^T
&=M_{\bar d,d}^{(d)}(1,0)^T
=(0,-1)^T,\\
M_{\bar Q,\bar d}^{(d)}(1,0)^T
&=M_{Q,d}^{(d)}(1,0)^T
=(0,+1)^T.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus the two within-source graphs are color-singlet diagonal on the tree
source, while **all singlet-to-octet mixing comes from the four cross-source
graphs**. The two cross-source color classes carry opposite octet signs;
their Dirac/momentum kernels must be kept distinct until the explicit
integrals establish any further relation.


## 4. Color-conservation check

The four external quark/antiquark legs form a total color singlet. Therefore
\[
\sum_{a<b}\mathbf T_a\cdot\mathbf T_b
=-\frac12\sum_a C_a
=-2C_F\mathbf1
=-\frac83\mathbf1.
\]
The six direct-basis matrices satisfy
\[
\boxed{\sum_{a<b}M_{ab}^{(d)}=-\frac83\mathbf1_{2\times2}.}
\]
This is an exact independent check of the direct-pairing color factors and their consistency with the crossed-basis matrices.

## 5. Choice for the QCD numerator calculation

For the first \(d^\dagger d\) QCD pilot, keep:
- the direct spinor pairing \((\bar Q,d)(\bar d,Q)\);
- the direct color basis \((C_{1,d},C_{8,d})\);
- the direct tree vector \((1,0)_d\);
- the direct-pairing scalar and tensor gamma structures generated by the six open-line numerators.

This avoids applying a d-dimensional spinor Fierz transformation before the UV poles are extracted. The crossed color basis remains a useful independent comparison, but it should not be mixed with direct-pairing spinor operators without transforming both spinor and color pairings consistently.
