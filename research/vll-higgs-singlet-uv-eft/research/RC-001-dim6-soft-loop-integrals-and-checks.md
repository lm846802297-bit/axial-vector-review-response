**Status:** the scalar bubble, the \(C_5C_5^\ast\) crossed two-fermion/two-scalar kernels, the \(C_5C_5^\ast\) four-fermion bubble, and the scalar-quartic insertion kernels have been reduced at integral level. Master v153 §§254–255 already contain separate bosonic field-strength/current-to-gauge and external-field/source calculations; this note does not duplicate them. The full one-loop dimension-six Wilson vector remains open because the fermionic current-current/gauge-correction amplitudes, their contact terms and counterterms, and the common-basis EOM/source ledger are not yet closed or independently generated by a diagram package.

**Baseline:** RC-001 PR #4, branch research/rc-001-uv-eft-difference-v13. The frozen model conventions are those of master §§243–245, 252, 284–286. The EFT retains \(H,S,A'\) and the light leptons. The heavy vector-like leptons \(E_\ell\) are integrated out.

## 1. Conventions and independently evaluated master integrals

Use \(d=4-2\epsilon\), the same \(\overline{\mathrm{MS}}\) convention on both sides, and define
\[
\Delta_{\rm UV,IR}=\frac1{\epsilon_{\rm UV,IR}}-\gamma_E+\ln4\pi,\qquad
L_\ell=\ln\frac{M_{E_\ell}^2}{\mu_R^2},\qquad
\ell_q=\ln\frac{-q^2-i0}{\mu_R^2}.
\]
The tree-level dimension-five coefficient is \(C_{5,\ell}=-y_\ell\lambda_\ell/M_{E_\ell}\). The dimension-six derivative coefficients are \(C_{LD,\ell}=|y_\ell|^2/M_{E_\ell}^2\) and \(C_{RD,\ell}=|\lambda_\ell|^2/M_{E_\ell}^2\).

The massless bubble is independently obtained from its Feynman-parameter representation:
\[
B_0(q^2;0,0)=\Delta_{\rm UV}-\ell_q+2,
\qquad
-\int_0^1 dx\,\ln[x(1-x)]=2.
\]
After shifting the loop momentum,
\[
\int\!\frac{d^dk}{(2\pi)^d}
\frac{k^\mu}{k^2(k+q)^2}
=-\frac{q^\mu}{2}\frac{i}{16\pi^2}
(\Delta_{\rm UV}-\ell_q+2),
\]
and replacing \(k^\mu\) by \((k+q)^\mu\) changes the sign. For the scalar four-point trace,
\[
2k\!\cdot\!(k+q)=k^2+(k+q)^2-q^2,
\]
so the two scaleless tadpoles vanish and the scalar kernel is \(-q^2B_0(q^2;0,0)\). This independently checks the EFT bubble formula in master §244.3; it does not independently generate the original UV diagram.

The hard-region master integrals are
\[
J_{22}\equiv\mu_R^{2\epsilon}\!\int\!\frac{d^dk}{(2\pi)^d}
\frac{1}{(k^2)^2(k^2-M^2)^2}
=\frac{i}{16\pi^2M^4}(-\Delta_{\rm IR}+L-2),
\]
\[
J_{13}\equiv\mu_R^{2\epsilon}\!\int\!\frac{d^dk}{(2\pi)^d}
\frac{1}{k^2(k^2-M^2)^3}
=\frac{i}{32\pi^2M^4},
\]
\[
K_{21}\equiv\mu_R^{2\epsilon}\!\int\!\frac{d^dk}{(2\pi)^d}
\frac{1}{(k^2)^2(k^2-M^2)}
=\frac{i}{16\pi^2M^2}(\Delta_{\rm IR}+1-L),
\qquad
J_{12}=-\frac{i}{16\pi^2M^2}.
\]
The Gamma-function/Feynman-parameter expansions are reproduced in calculations/RC-001-dim6-soft-loop-crosscheck.py. In particular, \(J_{22}\) has finite term \(-2\), \(K_{21}\) has finite term \(+1\), and \(J_{12}\), \(J_{13}\) are finite. These signs are not interchangeable: the single-heavy-propagator integral \(K_{21}\) has the opposite IR-pole sign to \(J_{22}\).

## 2. \(C_5C_5^\ast\) crossed two-fermion/two-scalar channels

Each effective \(C_5\) insertion contains one scalar pair and one chiral-fermion pair. With two external fermions and two external scalars, the soft loop contains one massless fermion and one massless scalar. Its stripped vector kernel is
\[
\mathcal I^\mu_{\rm soft}(q)
=\frac{1}{M_E^2}\int\!\frac{d^dk}{(2\pi)^d}
\frac{(k+q)^\mu}{k^2(k+q)^2}
=\frac{iq^\mu}{32\pi^2M_E^2}
(\Delta_{\rm UV}-\ell_q+2).
\]
Multiplying by \(|y_\ell\lambda_\ell|^2\) gives the expected \(|C_{5,\ell}|^2\) order.

### 2.1 Which channels have a hard region?

The four external-state assignments are not interchangeable:

| External state | Internal light lines | Hard-region classification |
|---|---|---|
| \(\bar L L H^\dagger H\) | \(\ell_R,S\) | The two heavy propagators carry only external-channel momenta. At bare-diagram level this topology has no independent hard loop-momentum region at \(O(M_E^{-2})\); its soft integral is reproduced by the EFT. Counterterm and field/operator renormalization matching still must be included. |
| \(\bar L L S^\dagger S\) | \(\ell_R,H\) | Both heavy propagators depend on the loop momentum; a hard region exists. |
| \(\bar\ell_R\ell_R H^\dagger H\) | \(L,S\) | Both heavy propagators depend on the loop momentum; a hard region exists. |
| \(\bar\ell_R\ell_R S^\dagger S\) | \(L,H\) | Both heavy propagators depend on the loop momentum; a hard region exists. The internal \(H,L\) doublet contraction gives a factor of two for this flavor-diagonal singlet channel. |

For the three loop-dependent channels, use the explicitly routed stripped kernel
\[
\mathcal I^\mu=M_E^2\!\int\!\frac{d^dk}{(2\pi)^d}
\frac{(k+q)^\mu}
{k^2(k+q)^2[(k+p_1)^2-M_E^2][(k-p_3)^2-M_E^2]},
\qquad q=p_1+p_2.
\]
Expanding the denominators to first order in external momenta and reducing \(k^\mu k^\nu\to g^{\mu\nu}k^2/d\) gives
\[
\mathcal I^\mu_{\rm hard}
=M_E^2\left[
\left(1-\frac2d\right)q^\mu J_{22}
+\frac2d(p_3-p_1)^\mu J_{13}
\right].
\]
Crucially, \(1-2/d=1/2-\epsilon/4+O(\epsilon^2)\). The \(O(\epsilon)\) term multiplies the IR pole and changes the finite constant. Keeping it gives
\[
\mathcal I^\mu_{\rm hard}
=\frac{i}{16\pi^2M_E^2}
\left[
q^\mu\left(-\frac12\Delta_{\rm IR}+\frac12L_E-\frac34\right)
+\frac14(p_3-p_1)^\mu
\right].
\]
Adding the soft region cancels the IR/UV poles:
\[
\mathcal I^\mu_{\rm hard}+\mathcal I^\mu_{\rm soft}
=\frac{i q^\mu}{32\pi^2M_E^2}
\left(L_E-\ell_q+\frac12\right)
+\frac{i(p_3-p_1)^\mu}{64\pi^2M_E^2}.
\]
The last term vanishes between massless on-shell external fermions by the Dirac equations. The hard local \(q^\mu\) coefficient is \(\tfrac12L_E-\tfrac34\), not the coefficient obtained by setting \(d=4\) before multiplying the pole.

For the current convention
\[
O_{\phi f}=(\phi^\dagger i\overleftrightarrow D_\mu\phi)
(\bar f\gamma^\mu f),
\]
the on-shell momentum projection obeys \(q_\mu\bar u\gamma^\mu u=\tfrac12(p_\phi-p_{\phi^\dagger})_\mu\bar u\gamma^\mu u\) when the barred fermion and \(\phi\) are at the same \(C_5\) vertex. Reversing to the conjugate vertex reverses this projection sign. Therefore, in this routing and operator convention, the finite hard current coefficients per flavor are
\[
C_{LS}^{\rm hard}
=\frac{|C_{5,\ell}|^2}{16\pi^2}
\left(\frac{L_\ell}{4}-\frac38\right),
\]
\[
C_{He}^{\rm hard}
=-\frac{|C_{5,\ell}|^2}{16\pi^2}
\left(\frac{L_\ell}{4}-\frac38\right),
\qquad
C_{Se}^{\rm hard}
=-2\frac{|C_{5,\ell}|^2}{16\pi^2}
\left(\frac{L_\ell}{4}-\frac38\right).
\]
For the flavor-resolved axial model, the operators in these per-flavor formulas are understood first with \(S_\ell\):
\[
O_{LS,\ell}^{(\ell)}=(S_\ell^\dagger i\overleftrightarrow D_\mu S_\ell)
(\bar L_\ell\gamma^\mu L_\ell),\qquad
O_{Se,\ell}^{(\ell)}=(S_\ell^\dagger i\overleftrightarrow D_\mu S_\ell)
(\bar\ell_R\gamma^\mu\ell_R),
\]
where \(S_\mu=S\), \(S_\tau=S^\ast\). Since
\[
S_\ell^\dagger i\overleftrightarrow D_\mu S_\ell
=\eta_\ell(S^\dagger i\overleftrightarrow D_\mu S),
\qquad \eta_\mu=+1,\quad\eta_\tau=-1,
\]
the displayed \(C_{LS,\ell}\) and \(C_{Se,\ell}\) are flavor-specific-current coefficients. If they are instead reported in a common-\(S\) basis, multiply each by \(\eta_\ell\). The \(O_{He}\) current uses \(H\) in the same orientation for both flavors and has no such factor. The \(O_{Se}\) factor of two is the internal electroweak-doublet multiplicity, separate from the \(\eta_\ell\) orientation sign. These are the Yukawa-box-sector hard projections only; they do not include the independent gauge corrections to the current operators or their full renormalization matrix.

The \(\bar L L H^\dagger H\) soft-only channel has the weak-index tensor \(H_i H^\dagger_j\,\bar L_i\gamma^\mu L_j\). It must be decomposed into singlet and triplet currents using
\[
\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}
=\frac12\left(\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}
+\sigma^I_{ij}\sigma^I_{kl}\right).
\]
It must not be represented by a singlet coefficient alone. The absence of a hard loop-momentum region for this particular bare topology does not remove the need to match the associated renormalization counterterms.

## 3. \(C_5C_5^\ast\) four-fermion channel

With external \(\bar L_i,\ell_R,\bar\ell_R,L_i\), the internal \(H\) and \(S\) scalar propagators form a bubble. The stripped soft and hard contributions are
\[
\mathcal I_{\rm soft}^{4f}
=\frac{i}{16\pi^2M_E^2}
(\Delta_{\rm UV}-\ell_q+2),
\qquad
\mathcal I_{\rm hard}^{4f}
=\frac{i}{16\pi^2M_E^2}
(-\Delta_{\rm IR}+L_E-2).
\]
The poles and constants cancel in their sum:
\[
\mathcal I_{\rm hard}^{4f}+\mathcal I_{\rm soft}^{4f}
=\frac{i}{16\pi^2M_E^2}
\ln\frac{M_E^2}{-q^2-i0}.
\]
Thus the hard local coefficient in the scalar-bilinear basis is
\[
C_{(\bar L\ell_R)(\bar\ell_R L)}
=\frac{|C_{5,\ell}|^2}{16\pi^2}(L_\ell-2)
\]
for the stated routing and overall-vertex convention. If converted to the usual vector-current \(O_{\ell e}\) basis, apply the chiral Fierz identity \((\bar L\ell_R)(\bar\ell_R L)=-\tfrac12(\bar L\gamma_\mu L)(\bar\ell_R\gamma^\mu\ell_R)\) with the same Grassmann ordering; do not mix scalar-bilinear and vector-current coefficients.

## 4. One \(Q_{LD}\) or \(Q_{RD}\) insertion plus a scalar-quartic vertex

For the off-shell two-fermion/two-scalar amplitude, let \(p\) and \(p'\) be the incoming barred/unbarred fermion momenta and \(Q=-(p+p')\) the total external scalar momentum. The derivative in \(Q_{LD}\) or \(Q_{RD}\) gives a stripped numerator \(-(k+p)^\mu\). The hard expansion contains \(K_{21}\) and \(J_{12}\):
\[
\mathcal I^\mu_{\rm hard}
=\frac{i}{16\pi^2M_E^2}
\left[
p^\mu(-\Delta_{\rm IR}+L_E-\tfrac32)
+Q^\mu(-\tfrac12\Delta_{\rm IR}+\tfrac12L_E-\tfrac34)
\right].
\]
The two coefficients have the required ratio, so this is proportional to \(p+Q/2=(p-p')/2\). The soft EFT loop is
\[
\mathcal I^\mu_{\rm soft}
=\frac{i}{16\pi^2M_E^2}
\left(p+\frac Q2\right)^\mu
(\Delta_{\rm UV}-\ell_Q+2).
\]
Hard and soft poles cancel and their sum is proportional to
\[
\left(p+\frac Q2\right)^\mu(L_E-\ell_Q+\tfrac12).
\]
Between massless on-shell fermions, \((p-p')_\mu\bar u\gamma^\mu u=0\). Thus this topology contributes to the **off-shell EOM/source sector**, not an independent on-shell current coefficient. Its local hard coefficient in the redundant operator \((\phi^\dagger\phi)\bar f i\overleftrightarrow{\slashed D}f\) is \(-\tfrac12 C_6\lambda/(16\pi^2)(L_E-\tfrac32)\) in the displayed derivative convention, before EOM reduction and before adding the other diagrams/counterterms in that operator sector.

The quartic contractions to retain are:
- \(Q_{LD}\) with \(\lambda_{HS}\): one singlet contraction in \(\bar L L S^\dagger S\);
- \(Q_{RD}\) with \(\lambda_{HS}\): one singlet contraction in \(\bar\ell_R\ell_R H^\dagger H\);
- \(Q_{RD}\) with \(\lambda_S\): factor \(4\lambda_S\) for the complex-singlet quartic;
- \(Q_{LD}\) with \(\lambda_H\): two weak-index tensors, each with factor \(2\lambda_H\), which must be kept separately until the singlet/triplet projection.

The first two contractions reduce through the fermion EOM to the corresponding singlet-scalar Yukawa-source operators in a generic theory. In the stated model, master §255.8 records \(\Xi_{L_\ell}^{(4)}=\Xi_{R_\ell}^{(4)}=0\) for the \(\mu,\tau\) fields because their ordinary renormalizable charged-lepton Yukawas are forbidden; consequently these EOM-only terms do not induce a nonzero dimension-six source for those flavors at this order. The \(\lambda_H\) contractions still require the full weak-index EOM projection. This note records the universal loop kernel and tensor multiplicities, not a complete quartic-dependent counterterm calculation.
