# RC-001 — 1PI matching boundary versus reducible external-leg W emissions

**Workstream:** RC-001-WW-UV-FF-02, Stage B2c-IVn-c  
**Purpose:** fix the object being matched before adding external-field W emissions to the ordinary-matter integrand. The local Yukawa-singlet endpoint charge sums are closed in IVn-b, but the connected five-point Green function also contains W emissions from external matter propagators. Those diagrams are one-particle reducible (1PR) and must not be silently added to the 1PI five-point vertex used to extract a local Wilson coefficient.

## 1. Two distinct objects

The 1PI five-point vertex is
\[
\Gamma^{(5)\,\alpha}_{W^a\bar L H H^\dagger L}
=\frac{\delta^5\Gamma_{\rm 1PI}}
{\delta W_\alpha^a\,\delta\bar L\,\delta H\,\delta H^\dagger\,\delta L}.
\]
Its internal ordinary-matter sub-block contains the matter-line insertions, scalar seagulls, and three-W graphs being assembled in B2c-IVl/IVm/IVn.

A connected Green function additionally contains diagrams in which the external W attaches to an external L or H propagator before that propagator enters the lower-point four-field subgraph. Cutting that external matter propagator separates the W vertex from the rest of the graph, so these are 1PR contributions. They are part of the connected Ward identity/LSZ organization, not independent local 1PI Wilson coefficients.

## 2. Universal external-leg Ward contraction

For a matter propagator \(G_\Phi\) and its gauge vertex \(V_\Phi^\alpha\), the external-leg contribution has the factorized form
\[
\mathcal E_\Phi^\alpha
=\Gamma^{(4)}(\ldots,p+q,\ldots)\,
G_\Phi(p+q)\,V_\Phi^\alpha(p+q,p)\,G_\Phi(p),
\]
with the ordering adjusted for an incoming versus outgoing field. The elementary Ward identity is
\[
q_\alpha V_\Phi^\alpha
=g_2T_\Phi^a\left[G_\Phi^{-1}(p+q)-G_\Phi^{-1}(p)\right].
\]
Consequently,
\[
G_\Phi(p+q)\,q_\alpha V_\Phi^\alpha\,G_\Phi(p)
=g_2T_\Phi^a\left[G_\Phi(p)-G_\Phi(p+q)\right].
\tag{2.1}
\]
For fermions, the two possible open-line orientations are the already checked \(P_L/P_R\) identities:
\[
S_F(p)\slashed qP_LS_F(p+q)
=S_F(p)P_R-P_LS_F(p+q),
\]
\[
S_F(p+q)\slashed qP_LS_F(p)
=P_LS_F(p)-S_F(p+q)P_R.
\]
For a scalar,
\[
\Delta(p+q)\,q\cdot(2p+q)\,\Delta(p)
=\Delta(p)-\Delta(p+q),
\qquad \Delta(p)=\frac1{p^2}.
\]
The signs reverse when the scalar momentum flow or charge-conjugate field orientation is reversed; the propagator ordering must be taken from the actual external leg.

## 3. Matching prescription

For Wilson matching, use the 1PI effective-action vertex on both the UV and EFT sides. The external-leg 1PR emissions are then not added to the local hard five-point coefficient. Their role is:
1. to complete the connected Green-function Ward identity;
2. to combine with the lower-point four-field amplitude and its gauge-covariant completion;
3. to be treated consistently with external-field wavefunction renormalization and LSZ.

If one instead compares connected amplitudes, the 1PR terms must be included on both sides. Their cancellation in the UV-minus-EFT difference is conditional on using the same external-field normalization and on matching the lower-point amplitude to the same order. It must not be assumed diagram by diagram without that lower-point matching.

## 4. Consequence for the current calculation

The present six-pair \(WW\) internal-attachment census is a candidate 1PI ordinary-matter sub-block. The external W attachments to the four external matter legs are recorded separately as 1PR/LSZ terms. This prevents double counting them in:
- the explicit \(R_{\rm FG}\) kernels;
- the scalar-line/seagull kernels;
- the three-W internal-attachment sector;
- the local EFT one-W contact generated by the covariant derivative.

The local Yukawa-singlet charge identities from IVn-b remain necessary: they fix the gauge transformation of the Yukawa-connected fields. They do not, by themselves, replace the external-leg 1PR terms in the connected Green function.

## 5. Acceptance boundary

Before claiming the complete connected STI, write the four external-leg emission factors with their actual incoming/outgoing momentum and field representations, and show how their inverse-propagator terms combine with the lower-point four-field amplitude. Before extracting a Wilson coefficient, keep those 1PR terms outside the 1PI matching difference and include the corresponding field-renormalization convention.

No external-leg numerical amplitude, complete connected STI, ghost completion, loop integral, or Wilson coefficient is claimed in this note.
