# RC-001 — UV external-\(W\) longitudinal Ward contraction for the six internal-\(W\) subgraphs

**Workstream:** RC-001-WW-UV-FF-02, Stage A  
**Status:** exact line-level Ward identities, propagator telescoping, six-pair \(SU(2)_L\) contractions, and the non-Abelian internal-\(W\) contraction have been checked. This is the longitudinal Ward-identity reduction needed to organize the UV five-point calculation. It is **not** the integrated UV five-point form factor and does not close the full Slavnov–Taylor identity.

## 1. Conventions and the distinction from the previous longitudinal check

Use the all-incoming four-point ordering
\[
\bar L_i(p_1),\quad H_j(p_2),\quad H^\dagger_k(p_3),\quad L_l(p_4),
\qquad p_1+p_2+p_3+p_4=0.
\]
For the five-point amplitude, the external \(W^a(q)\) is also incoming, so the four matter momenta sum to \(-q\). The momentum \(q\) is the **external** \(W\) momentum; \(k\) denotes the loop momentum of the **internal** \(W\) exchanged in LD-W-01…06.

The matching is performed in the unbroken \(SU(2)_L\) theory. The light doublet \(L\), the Higgs doublet \(H\), and the \(W\) are massless for this sub-block. The heavy vector-like lepton \(E\) is an \(SU(2)_L\) singlet, so there is no \(WEE\) vertex.

A terminology warning is important. The earlier check
\[
D_{\mu\nu}(k)\supset (1-\zeta)\frac{k_\mu k_\nu}{(k^2)^2}
\]
contracted the **internal-\(W\) propagator momentum** \(k\) and tested gauge-parameter dependence of the four-point cross-box subset. The present audit contracts the **new external-\(W\) momentum** \(q_\mu\) at the five-point insertion vertices. These are different tests.

## 2. Matter-line Ward identities

For a massless left-handed fermion doublet, with the chiral projector understood on the weak-current vertex,
\[
S_F^{-1}(p)=\slashed p,\qquad
q_\mu\Gamma_F^{a\mu}(p+q,p)
=g_2t^a\big[S_F^{-1}(p+q)-S_F^{-1}(p)\big].
\]
Thus
\[
S_F(p+q)\,q_\mu\Gamma_F^{a\mu}\,S_F(p)
=g_2\big[t^aS_F(p)-S_F(p+q)t^a\big].
\tag{2.1}
\]
The explicit four-dimensional Dirac-matrix check verifies (2.1) for all 16 matrix components at generic symbolic \(p,q\).

For a complex Higgs doublet, the scalar gauge vertex obeys
\[
q_\mu\Gamma_H^{a\mu}(p+q,p)
=g_2t^a\big[(p+q)^2-p^2\big],
\]
because
\[
q\cdot(2p+q)=(p+q)^2-p^2.
\]
With \(\Delta_H(p)=1/p^2\),
\[
\Delta_H(p+q)\,q_\mu\Gamma_H^{a\mu}\,\Delta_H(p)
=g_2\big[t^a\Delta_H(p)-\Delta_H(p+q)t^a\big].
\tag{2.2}
\]
A scalar mass, if retained as an infrared regulator, cancels from the inverse-propagator difference.

Equations (2.1) and (2.2) have the same endpoint form. For a line containing several propagator segments, summing the external-\(W\) insertion over every allowed segment gives a telescoping product:
\[
\sum_{j=1}^{n}
\left(\prod_{i<j}G_i(q)\right)
\big[G_j(0)-G_j(q)\big]
\left(\prod_{i>j}G_i(0)\right)
=
\prod_{i=1}^{n}G_i(0)-\prod_{i=1}^{n}G_i(q).
\tag{2.3}
\]
The exact symbolic audit verifies (2.3) for chain lengths \(n=1,\ldots,6\). Consequently, the longitudinal insertion does not require a new loop integration for every position on a fixed matter line: it reduces to endpoint terms. The endpoint terms must still be assembled at the Yukawa vertices and at the internal gauge-boson endpoints.

The matching is off shell. Therefore the external inverse-propagator terms generated by this procedure must be retained until the common UV–EFT subtraction and the declared amputation/LSZ convention are applied. One must not set them to zero by imposing external Dirac equations prematurely.

## 3. The six internal-\(W\) pair classes

The existing six-pair census is retained without recomputing its four-point integrals. The exact weak tensors are:

| Pair | Internal-\(W\) endpoints | Weak tensor | External-\(W\) matter-line insertions |
|---|---|---:|---|
| LD-W-01 | \(\bar L-L\) | \(-T_3/4\) | both fermion paths |
| LD-W-02 | \(\bar L-H^\dagger\) | \(+T_3/4\) | one fermion and one scalar path |
| LD-W-03 | \(\bar L-H\) | \(-3T_0/4\) | one fermion and one scalar path; same Yukawa vertex |
| LD-W-04 | \(L-H^\dagger\) | \(-3T_0/4\) | one fermion and one scalar path; same conjugate Yukawa vertex |
| LD-W-05 | \(L-H\) | \(+T_3/4\) | one fermion and one scalar path |
| LD-W-06 | \(H^\dagger-H\) | \(-T_3/4\) | both scalar paths |

Here
\[
T_0=\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl},\qquad
T_3=\sum_I\sigma^I_{ij}\sigma^I_{kl}
=2\delta_{il}\delta_{kj}-\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}.
\]
The pairwise generator contractions are reproduced for all six classes and all 16 weak-index components in the new exact audit. The total \(T_0\) and \(T_3\) tensors are separately \(SU(2)_L\)-invariant.

At the two Yukawa vertices, gauge invariance gives
\[
(t_{\bar L}^a+t_H^a)T_0=0,\qquad
(t_{H^\dagger}^a+t_L^a)T_0=0.
\tag{3.1}
\]
The audit verifies these identities generator by generator and component by component. They explain an important feature of the telescoping sum: the boundary terms at a Yukawa vertex cancel only after the external-\(W\) insertions on **both** fields belonging to that vertex have been included. A single attachment graph, or a single line-pair class considered in isolation, is not expected to satisfy the Ward identity.

For LD-W-03/04, the same-vertex pure-soft result remains unchanged. Their \(O(M_E^{-2})\) light-loop form factors are reproduced by the EFT \(Q_{LD}\) insertion, and their hard-minus-EFT contribution is zero. They must nevertheless remain in the complete Ward/contact sum. For LD-W-01/02/05/06, the existing nonexceptional hard polynomial and soft subtraction are retained.

The complete five-point set for each pair must include:
1. external-\(W\) insertions on every internal light-fermion segment;
2. external-\(W\) insertions on every internal Higgs segment;
3. the scalar two-gauge seagull from \((D_\mu H)^\dagger D^\mu H\) whenever the internal and external gauge fields meet on a scalar line;
4. the external-\(W\) insertion on the internal-\(W\) propagator through the three-\(W\) vertex;
5. external-leg emissions and the associated amputation/LSZ terms.

The scalar seagull is not optional: it is required for the two-gauge scalar Ward identity. The internal-\(W\) insertion is also not optional: the matter-line telescoping leaves an adjoint-gauge endpoint term which is completed by the non-Abelian gauge vertex.

## 4. Longitudinal contraction of the internal-\(W\) line

For the stripped Yang–Mills vertex with all incoming momenta \((q,k,-k-q)\), the exact contraction is
\[
q^\alpha\Gamma_{\alpha\mu\nu}^{abc}(q,k,-k-q)
=
g_2f^{abc}
\left[\mathcal K_{\mu\nu}(k+q)-\mathcal K_{\mu\nu}(k)\right],
\tag{4.1}
\]
where
\[
\mathcal K_{\mu\nu}(k)=k^2g_{\mu\nu}-k_\mu k_\nu.
\]
The new symbolic audit checks all 16 Lorentz components of (4.1).

There is a subtle but important gauge-fixing qualification. In ordinary Feynman gauge,
\[
D^{-1}_{F,\mu\nu}(k)=k^2g_{\mu\nu},
\]
so the right-hand side of (4.1) is **not** merely the difference of Feynman-gauge inverse propagators. Explicitly,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal K_{\mu\nu}(k+q)-\mathcal K_{\mu\nu}(k)
={}&\big[(k+q)^2-k^2\big]g_{\mu\nu}\\
&-\big[(k+q)_\mu(k+q)_\nu-k_\mu k_\nu\big].
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.2}
\]
The second line is nonzero for generic \(q,k\). It is the longitudinal/gauge-fixing remainder that must be treated in the full Slavnov–Taylor completion, together with the appropriate ghost and gauge-fixing structures. It cannot be dropped by replacing \(\mathcal K_{\mu\nu}\) with \(k^2g_{\mu\nu}\).

This distinction is especially relevant here because the existing four-point sub-block was evaluated in conventional Feynman gauge. The new audit identifies the exact remainder; it does not claim that the ghost/gauge-fixing part has already been canceled.

## 5. Resulting UV–EFT Ward relation and the existing EFT contact

After summing all matter-line insertion positions, all scalar seagull terms generated by the covariant Higgs kinetic term, the internal-\(W\) three-gauge vertex, and the external-leg terms, the longitudinal five-point identity has the schematic off-shell form
\[
q_\mu\Gamma_{\mathrm{UV},5}^{a\mu}
=
g_2\,\delta_{\mathrm{ext}}^a\Gamma_{\mathrm{UV},4}
+\mathcal R_{\mathrm{ST}}^a.
\tag{5.1}
\]
Here \(\delta_{\mathrm{ext}}^a\) denotes the sum of the \(SU(2)_L\) generators acting on the four external representations, with the momentum shifts induced by \(q\). The term \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{ST}}^a\) denotes the gauge-fixing/ghost and off-shell inverse-propagator terms appropriate to the chosen conventional gauge. Equation (5.1) is not a statement that the amputated off-shell contraction vanishes.

Using the same regulator, momentum routing, gauge fixing, and field convention on the EFT side gives
\[
q_\mu\big(\Gamma_{\mathrm{UV},5}^{a\mu}
-\Gamma_{\mathrm{EFT},5}^{a\mu}\big)
=
g_2\,\delta_{\mathrm{ext}}^a
\big(\Gamma_{\mathrm{UV},4}-\Gamma_{\mathrm{EFT},4}\big)
+\Delta\mathcal R_{\mathrm{ST}}^a.
\tag{5.2}
\]
The nonlocal soft terms must cancel in the common UV–EFT difference. The remaining local longitudinal contact is therefore fixed by the gauge-covariant completion of the four-point hard polynomial.

For reference, the existing internal-\(W\) hard polynomial is written in the redundant off-shell basis as
\[
C_{\mathrm{hard}}=F(a\,v_D+b\,v_-+c\,v_+),\qquad
F=\frac{g_2^2|y_\ell|^2}{16\pi^2M_E^2},
\]
with
\[
a=-\frac12+\frac L2,\quad
b=-\frac1{32}+\frac{7L}{16},\quad
c=\frac7{32}-\frac{5L}{16},\qquad
L=\ln\frac{M_E^2}{\mu_R^2}.
\]
Its local EFT one-\(W\) contact has already been constructed in the existing EFT Ward note:
\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{EFT}}^{(1W)}
=Fg_2W_\mu^J\left[
ia\,\epsilon^{IJK}(\bar L\sigma^I H)\gamma^\mu(H^\dagger\sigma^K L)
+2b\,T_{3(ij,kl)}(\bar L_i\gamma^\mu L_l)
H_k^\dagger\left(\frac{\sigma^J}{2}\right)_{jm}H_m
\right].
\tag{5.3}
\]
The \(c\) direction has no direct \(W\) contact in this redundant representation. At \(\mu_R=M_E\), the three contact coefficients, including \(F\), are
\[
\boxed{\left(-\frac{i g_2F}{2},\ -\frac{g_2F}{16},\ 0\right).}
\]
The new script independently reproduces these coefficients. This is the EFT-side target for the UV longitudinal calculation, not a UV five-point result.
