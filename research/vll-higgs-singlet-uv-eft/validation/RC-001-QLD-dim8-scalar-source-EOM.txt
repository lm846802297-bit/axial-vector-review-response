RC-001 QLD8 scalar-source higher-derivative EOM audit

Let K=-D^2 and K_H=f_H H+J_H, with
  f_H=-mu_H^2+2 lambda_H X+lambda_HS Y,
  D^2 H=-f_H H-J_H.

Since K is self-adjoint in the action,
  H^dagger K^3 H = (K H)^dagger K(K H)
  ~= K_H^dagger(-D^2)K_H
  ~= (D_mu K_H)^dagger D^mu K_H.
Thus
  O_S ~= |D_mu(f_H H+J_H)|^2.

Product rule:
  D_mu K_H=(partial_mu f_H)H+f_H D_mu H+D_mu J_H.

Full source decomposition:
  |D K_H|^2 =
      X (partial f_H)^2
    + f_H^2 |D H|^2
    + (D J_H)^dagger(D J_H)
    + f_H (partial_mu f_H)(partial^mu X)
    + (partial_mu f_H)[H^dagger D^mu J_H+(D^mu J_H)^dagger H]
    + f_H[(D_mu H)^dagger D^mu J_H+(D^mu J_H)^dagger D_mu H].

Pure-bosonic expansion:
  f_H^2 =
      mu_H^4 - 4 mu_H^2 lambda_H X
      - 2 mu_H^2 lambda_HS Y
      + 4 lambda_H^2 X^2
      + 4 lambda_H lambda_HS X Y
      + lambda_HS^2 Y^2.

  X(partial f_H)^2+f_H(partial f_H).(partial X) =
      [-2 mu_H^2 lambda_H + 8 lambda_H^2 X
       + 2 lambda_H lambda_HS Y] (partial X)^2
    + [-mu_H^2 lambda_HS + 6 lambda_H lambda_HS X
       + lambda_HS^2 Y] partial X.partial Y
    + lambda_HS^2 X (partial Y)^2.

Source companions are all retained:
  (partial f_H)[H^dagger D J_H+h.c.]
  f_H[(D H)^dagger D J_H+h.c.]
  (D J_H)^dagger D J_H.

The renormalizable SM Yukawa source J_H^(4) contributes at this order. The dimension-five C5 source is suppressed by one additional 1/M_E and is beyond the strict M_E^-4 coefficient order; it is not set to zero.
