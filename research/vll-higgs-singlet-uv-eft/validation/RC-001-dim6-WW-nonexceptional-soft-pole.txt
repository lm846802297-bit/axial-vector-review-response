RC-001 nonexceptional pure-WW EFT-soft audit

Kinematics:
  p_i^2 = mu_IR^2
  p_i.p_j = -mu_IR^2/3 (i != j)
  p1+p2+p3+p4 = 0

General routing:
  q_E = k-p1-p2
  Pair 01 shifts: a=-p1, b=p4
  Pair 02 shifts: a=-p1, b=p3
  Pair 05 shifts: a=-p2, b=p4
  Pair 06 shifts: a=-p2, b=p3
  All four have a^2=b^2=mu_IR^2 and (a-b)^2=4 mu_IR^2/3.
  Setting p1=p3=0, p2=P, p4=-P reproduces all four exceptional denominators in the prior routing note.

Direct EFT insertion comparison:
  The Q_LD derivative momentum is q_D=p3+p4+k.
  Momentum conservation gives q_D=k-p1-p2, equal to the soft limit of the UV heavy momentum.
  Substituting q_D for q_E makes the EFT numerator equal the UV numerator after the soft heavy expansion for all four pairs (zero symbolic residuals). This is an algebraic routing identity, not an independent diagram-generation check.

Exact four-dimensional Euclidean Clifford/SymPy residue:
  LD-W-01: (-1, 0, -1) in (slash p1, slash p2, slash p3)
  LD-W-02: (0, -1, 3/2)
  LD-W-05: (0, 3/2, 1/2)
  LD-W-06: (-1, -1, 0)

  Weak-triplet weights: (-1,+1,+1,-1)/4
  Weighted residue before the leading heavy factor:
    R_T3 = (1/2) slash p1 + (3/8) slash p2 + (3/4) slash p3.

  Leading soft heavy propagator: -1/M_E^2.
  Stripped EFT soft UV pole:
    -R_T3 / (16 pi^2 M_E^2 epsilon_UV)
  Multiply by the common g2^2 |y_l|^2 and overall Feynman-rule phase.
  Exceptional limit: R_T3 -> (3/8) slash P, reproducing the previous 3/8 coefficient.

Tensor-basis finding:
  The local UV pole has only the three independent slash-p_i structures.
  The finite nonlocal soft part contains a nonzero rank-three antisymmetric-gamma
  component. In four-dimensional chiral projection it is dual to slash(p_perp) P_L,
  p_perp^mu = epsilon^{mu nu rho sigma} p1_nu p2_rho p3_sigma.
  Retain gamma^[mu nu rho] in d dimensions until after UV-EFT subtraction.
