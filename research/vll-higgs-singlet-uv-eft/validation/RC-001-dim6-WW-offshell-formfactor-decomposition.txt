RC-001 nonexceptional hard off-shell momentum decomposition

The finite local hard polynomial is
  C = (1/2-L/2) p1 +(11/16-3L/8) p2 +(1/4-3L/4) p3,
  L=ln(M_E^2/mu_R^2).

Choose the independent momentum vectors
  v_D = p3+p4 = -(p1+p2),
  v_minus = p2-p3,
  v_plus = p2+p3.

Exact SymPy decomposition:
  C = a v_D + b v_minus + c v_plus,
  a = -1/2 + L/2,
  b = -1/32 + 7L/16,
  c = 7/32 - 5L/16.

At mu_R=M_E:
  (a,b,c)=(-1/2,-1/32,7/32).

Checks:
  PASS: exact reconstruction of all three independent p_i coefficients.
  PASS: exceptional limit p1=p3=0, p2=P gives 11/16-3L/8.

Interpretation boundary:
  v_D is the derivative momentum of the triplet Q_LD^(3) representative.
  v_minus and v_plus are retained as independent off-shell form-factor
  directions at this stage. They are not yet assigned to Warsaw-basis
  operators: weak-index contractions, total derivatives, EOM/source terms,
  and the complete amplitude sum must be included before such an assignment.
