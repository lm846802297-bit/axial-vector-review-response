# Low-x B.13 local-kernel pilot (partial)

The full B.2 energy grid reaches a relevant minimum path-local coordinate
\[
x_{\min}=\frac{m_{A'}/N_{\max}-m_{A'}}{T_{\max}}=0.0221811.
\]
The current Global-03 interpolation table starts at x=0.5, so it cannot yet support full-band B.2 transport.

A high-order local pilot evaluated x = {0.01, 0.025, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5} at r = 8.482141 km and x = 0.01 at r = 12.5 km. All seven completed points pass the 0.1% successive-order criterion. The maximum final change is 0.03292%; detailed-balance residuals are below 6.2e-16 and final-muon shell residuals below 1.63e-13.

The pilot was planned for 24 points but timed out after seven. This is not a global low-x interpolation scan. The next prerequisite is a low-x extension beginning at x=0.01 across the radial profile, followed by merging with the existing x>=0.5 table. Until then, only the 210–510 MeV high-energy-band B.2 result is claimed.
