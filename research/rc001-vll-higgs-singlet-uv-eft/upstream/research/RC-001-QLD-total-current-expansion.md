# RC-001 QLD8 gauge-EOM total-current expansion for the axial model

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Workstream:** PR #11, QLD8 off-shell IBP/EOM reduction  
**Scope:** expand the gauge-EOM currents appearing in the non-Abelian commutator map into the complete leading-renormalizable light-field current basis of the original one-VEV axial \(U_A(1)_{L_\mu-L_\tau}\) model. This is a current/operator bookkeeping result, not a loop calculation or final Wilson-vector closure.

## 1. Conventions and EFT field content

Use the upstream background-field convention
\[
D_\mu^+=\partial_\mu+i g'YB_\mu+i g_2T^aW_\mu^a+i g_XxA_\mu^X,
\]
with \(Y_H=1/2\), \(T^a=\sigma^a/2\), and normalized axial charges
\[
x(L_\mu)=+1,\quad x(\mu_R)=-1,\quad
x(L_\tau)=-1,\quad x(\tau_R)=+1,\quad x(S)=+2.
\]
The original axial coupling is \(g_X=g_{A'}Q_\ell\). The Higgs, electron sector, and quarks have \(x=0\). The tau EFT operator contains \(S_\tau=S^*\).

The currents below are those of the low-energy EFT **after** integrating out \(E_\mu,E_\tau\). The heavy VLLs therefore do not appear as explicit matter-current terms. Their threshold effects belong to the matched gauge parameters and higher-dimensional operators. The minimal original model has no right-handed neutrinos; the neutrino components of \(L_e,L_\mu,L_\tau\) are included in the left-doublet currents. No optional \(\sigma+3N_R\) completion is imported.

Define
\[
X=H^\dagger H,\qquad X^a=H^\dagger T^aH,
\]
\[
j_H^\mu=iH^\dagger\overleftrightarrow D^\mu H,\qquad
j_H^{a\mu}=iH^\dagger T^a\overleftrightarrow D^\mu H,\qquad
j_S^\mu=iS^\dagger\overleftrightarrow D^\mu S.
\]
All derivatives in these currents are the same covariant derivative used in the action. Color sums are implicit in quark bilinears.

## 2. Complete leading-renormalizable matter currents

### 2.1 Hypercharge

The total hypercharge current is
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
J_Y^\mu={}&\frac12j_H^\mu
+\sum_{i=1}^{3}\bigg[
\frac16\bar Q_{Li}\gamma^\mu Q_{Li}
+\frac23\bar u_{Ri}\gamma^\mu u_{Ri}
-\frac13\bar d_{Ri}\gamma^\mu d_{Ri}\\
&\hspace{2.4cm}
-\frac12\bar L_{Li}\gamma^\mu L_{Li}
-\bar e_{Ri}\gamma^\mu e_{Ri}
\bigg].
\end{aligned}}
\]
Here \(Q_{Li}\) and \(L_{Li}\) are the three SM left-handed weak doublets, and \(u_{Ri},d_{Ri},e_{Ri}\) are the right-handed singlets. The \(\mu,\tau\) entries carry their usual SM hypercharges; their different axial \(x\) charges do not alter \(J_Y^\mu\).

For later separation write
\[
J_Y^\mu=Y_Hj_H^\mu+J_{Y,f}^\mu,\qquad Y_H=\frac12,
\]
where \(J_{Y,f}\) is the complete fermionic sum displayed above.

### 2.2 Weak isospin

The total \(SU(2)_L\) current is
\[
\boxed{
J_2^{a\mu}=j_H^{a\mu}
+\sum_{i=1}^{3}\left[
\bar Q_{Li}\gamma^\mu T^aQ_{Li}
+\bar L_{Li}\gamma^\mu T^aL_{Li}
\right].
}
\]
Only left-handed weak doublets contribute. Right-handed charged leptons and quarks, \(S\), and the VLL singlets do not carry \(SU(2)_L\). Write
\[
J_2^{a\mu}=j_H^{a\mu}+J_{2,f}^{a\mu}.
\]

### 2.3 Axial \(U(1)_X\)

With the normalized charges above, the low-energy \(U(1)_X\) current is
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
J_X^\mu={}&2j_S^\mu
+\bar L_\mu\gamma^\mu L_\mu
-\bar\mu_R\gamma^\mu\mu_R\\
&-\bar L_\tau\gamma^\mu L_\tau
+\bar\tau_R\gamma^\mu\tau_R.
\end{aligned}}
\]
The doublet terms include \(\nu_{\mu L}\) and \(\nu_{\tau L}\). The electron and quark sectors are neutral. The \(S\) term carries charge \(x(S)=2\). The heavy \(E_\mu,E_\tau\) terms are absent in this EFT current because those fields have been integrated out.

For the upstream \(+ig_XxA_X\) convention and gauge kinetic term \(-F_X^2/4\), the leading gauge equations are
\[
\partial_\rho B^{\rho\mu}=g'J_Y^\mu,\qquad
(\mathcal D_\rho^{\rm adj}W^{\rho\mu})^a=g_2J_2^{a\mu},\qquad
\partial_\rho F_X^{\rho\mu}=g_XJ_X^\mu.
\]
Under the axial-paper raw-field convention \(D^-=\partial-igA^-\) with \(A^+=-A^-\), the same currents are obtained but each raw-field equation has the opposite sign:
\[
\partial_\rho B_-^{\rho\mu}=-g'J_Y^\mu,\qquad
(\mathcal D_{\rho,-}^{\rm adj}W_-^{\rho\mu})^a=-g_2J_2^{a\mu},\qquad
\partial_\rho F_{X,-}^{\rho\mu}=-g_XJ_X^\mu.
\]
This is the field-variable map recorded in the axial PR #69 interface; do not mix the two EOM signs in one operator reduction.

## 3. Explicit current representatives generated by the QLD8 commutators

The non-Abelian note gives the following gauge-EOM representatives. Substituting the complete currents above makes their matter content explicit.

Let
\[
(\mathcal D_\nu^{\rm adj}X)^a
=\partial_\nu X^a-g_2\epsilon^{abc}W_\nu^bX^c.
\]

### 3.1 External Abelian background \(F_A=B\) or \(F_X\)

The internal weak-field commutator gives
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal O_{DW}^{A\leftarrow W}
\longrightarrow
-\frac{g_2^2}{2}F_A^{\mu\nu}
\left(j_{H,\mu}^b+J_{2,f,\mu}^b\right)
(\mathcal D_\nu^{\rm adj}X)^b.
}
\]
The internal hypercharge commutator gives
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathcal O_{\partial B}^{A}
&\longrightarrow
-\frac{g'^2Y_H}{2}F_A^{\mu\nu}
\left(Y_Hj_{H,\mu}+J_{Y,f,\mu}\right)\partial_\nu X\\
&=
-\frac{g'^2}{4}F_A^{\mu\nu}
\left(\frac12j_{H,\mu}+J_{Y,f,\mu}\right)\partial_\nu X.
\end{aligned}}
\]
These expressions apply to external \(B\) and external \(F_X\). In the \(F_X\) case, \(H\) has no direct \(X\) connection, but the mixed \(F_XB\) and \(F_XW\) contacts remain because the internal SM curvature acts on \(H\).

### 3.2 External weak background \(W^a_{\mu\nu}\)

The same-group weak-current term becomes
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathcal O_{DW}^{W^a}\longrightarrow{}&
-\frac{g_2^2}{8}W^{a\mu\nu}
\left(j_{H,\mu}^a+J_{2,f,\mu}^a\right)\partial_\nu X\\
&+\frac{g_2^2}{4}W^{a\mu\nu}\epsilon^{abc}
\left(j_{H,\mu}^b+J_{2,f,\mu}^b\right)j_{H,\nu}^c.
\end{aligned}}
\]
The mixed hypercharge-current term is
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathcal O_{\partial B}^{W^a}\longrightarrow
-\frac{g'^2}{4}W^{a\mu\nu}
\left(\frac12j_{H,\mu}+J_{Y,f,\mu}\right)
(\mathcal D_\nu^{\rm adj}X)^a.
\end{aligned}}
\]

These are the complete leading-renormalizable matter-current substitutions for the restricted commutator representatives in the upstream note. They are not yet a nonredundant dimension-eight basis: the fermion-current operators still require the declared fermion-EOM/Fierz and flavor-basis treatment.

## 4. EOM replacement is an alternative, not an additional term

For every derivative-of-field-strength representative, choose exactly one of:
- retain \(\partial_\rho F^{\rho\mu}\) or \((\mathcal D_\rho^{\rm adj}W^{\rho\mu})^a\) as a gauge-EOM operator; or
- replace it by the complete current shown above.

Do not retain both representations in the same basis. The Higgs-current part is already included in \(J_Y\) and \(J_2\); adding a separate Higgs-only current term after substituting the total current would double count it.

The currents here are the leading renormalizable EFT currents. Higher-dimensional corrections to the gauge EOM must be included only if the declared inverse-\(M_E\) power counting promotes them to the same order as the operator being reduced. In the present \(O(M_E^{-4})\) commutator reduction, inserting the \(O(M_E^{-2})\) gauge-current correction from a dimension-six EFT operator would start at \(O(M_E^{-6})\), outside the retained order.

## 5. Validation and limitations

The companion exact-rational bookkeeping audit checks:
1. the complete SM hypercharge assignments and the \(Y_H=1/2\) Higgs coefficient;
2. the complete set of \(SU(2)_L\) doublet currents and the absence of right-handed singlet currents;
3. the normalized \(U(1)_X\) charges of \(L_\mu,\mu_R,L_\tau,\tau_R,S\);
4. exclusion of \(E_\mu,E_\tau\) from the low-energy EFT currents;
5. the rational prefactors \(Y_H/2=1/4\), \(1/8\), \(1/4\), and \(1/2\) in the four EOM substitutions;
6. the opposite raw-field EOM sign in the axial-paper convention.

The audit is a charge/current bookkeeping check. It does not evaluate any loop integral, perform the fermion-EOM/Fierz reduction, close the two-background matching, or establish a complete dimension-eight Wilson vector. The full UV–EFT matching and independent diagram-level validation remain open.
