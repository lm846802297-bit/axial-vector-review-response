# RC-001 QLD8 — P1.5c+ D-dimensional direct-pairing Dirac-rank closure

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Stage:** P1.5c+ — formal D-dimensional numerator closure audit  
**Status:** the four cross-source exchange chains are shown to close exactly on SS, TS, ST and TT in the original direct spinor pairing for arbitrary D. The two same-bilinear exchange chains also reduce exactly to rank zero plus rank two on their single affected bilinear. This is a Dirac-rank closure statement for the six listed one-gluon Feynman-gauge numerator chains, not a complete gauge-covariant dimension-eight operator-basis closure.

## 1. Scope and conventions

Keep the original direct pairing
\[
(\bar Q,d)\otimes(\bar d,Q)
\]
and \(D\)-dimensional Clifford algebra
\[
\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu},\qquad
\Gamma^{\mu\nu}=\gamma^{[\mu}\gamma^{\nu]}.
\]
The exact identity
\[
\boxed{\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu=g^{\mu\nu}+\Gamma^{\mu\nu}}
\]
holds for arbitrary D. Since the chiral projectors commute with every even gamma string, no four-dimensional duality or Fierz identity is needed in the following decomposition.

The four cross-source chains have two gamma matrices on each original bilinear. Their momentum factors are \(x_\alpha y_\beta\), with the left/right orientation fixed in the P1.4b1 note.

## 2. Exact D-dimensional decomposition of all four cross-source chains

Expanding each two-gamma product with the identity above gives:

### Topology 1: \((\bar Q,Q)\)
\[
\begin{aligned}
(\gamma^\rho\gamma^\alpha P_R)\otimes
(P_L\gamma^\beta\gamma_\rho)
={}&g^{\alpha\beta}P_R\otimes P_L\\
&+\Gamma^{\beta\alpha}P_R\otimes P_L
+P_R\otimes\Gamma^{\beta\alpha}P_L\\
&+(\Gamma^{\rho\alpha}P_R)\otimes
(\Gamma^\beta{}_\rho P_L).
\end{aligned}
\]

### Topology 2: \((\bar Q,\bar d)\)
\[
\begin{aligned}
(\gamma^\rho\gamma^\alpha P_R)\otimes
(\gamma_\rho\gamma^\beta P_L)
={}&g^{\alpha\beta}P_R\otimes P_L\\
&+\Gamma^{\beta\alpha}P_R\otimes P_L
+P_R\otimes\Gamma^{\alpha\beta}P_L\\
&+(\Gamma^{\rho\alpha}P_R)\otimes
(\Gamma_\rho{}^\beta P_L).
\end{aligned}
\]

### Topology 3: \((Q,d)\)
\[
\begin{aligned}
(P_R\gamma^\alpha\gamma^\rho)\otimes
(P_L\gamma^\beta\gamma_\rho)
={}&g^{\alpha\beta}P_R\otimes P_L\\
&+\Gamma^{\alpha\beta}P_R\otimes P_L
+P_R\otimes\Gamma^{\beta\alpha}P_L\\
&+(\Gamma^{\alpha\rho}P_R)\otimes
(\Gamma^\beta{}_\rho P_L).
\end{aligned}
\]

### Topology 4: \((\bar d,d)\)
\[
\begin{aligned}
(P_R\gamma^\alpha\gamma^\rho)\otimes
(\gamma_\rho\gamma^\beta P_L)
={}&g^{\alpha\beta}P_R\otimes P_L\\
&+\Gamma^{\alpha\beta}P_R\otimes P_L
+P_R\otimes\Gamma^{\alpha\beta}P_L\\
&+(\Gamma^{\alpha\rho}P_R)\otimes
(\Gamma_\rho{}^\beta P_L).
\end{aligned}
\]

These are algebraic identities in arbitrary D. The four terms in each line are precisely:
\[
(0,0),\quad(2,0),\quad(0,2),\quad(2,2).
\]
No \(\Gamma_{(3)}\), \(\Gamma_{(4)}\), or higher-rank string is generated by these cross-source numerator chains.

The explicit 4D matrix audit already in the repository remains an independent check of the index ordering and signs. It is not used as the proof of the D-dimensional statement; the proof is the exact Clifford decomposition above.

## 3. Relative signs and color-weighted sectors

Using \(\Gamma^{\beta\alpha}=-\Gamma^{\alpha\beta}\), the chain signs relative to the common SS, TS, ST and TT seeds are:

| Topology | SS | TS | ST | TT |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \((\bar Q,Q)\) | + | − | − | − |
| \((\bar Q,\bar d)\) | + | − | + | + |
| \((Q,d)\) | + | + | − | + |
| \((\bar d,d)\) | + | + | + | − |

Multiplication by the direct-color tree-singlet weights \((-1,+1,+1,-1)\) gives:
\[
SS=(-,+,+,-),\quad
TS=(+,-,+,-),\quad
ST=(+,+,-,-),\quad
TT=(+,+,+,+).
\]
This reproduces the independently fixed P1.5a signs. In particular, the four TT integrals add after the color and chain signs are combined.

## 4. The two same-bilinear chains also have rank at most two

For a unit-weight antisymmetrized rank-n gamma string,
\[
\gamma^\rho\Gamma_{(n)}\gamma_\rho
=(-1)^n(D-2n)\Gamma_{(n)}.
\]
For \(n=0,2\), this gives
\[
\gamma^\rho\gamma_\rho=D,\qquad
\gamma^\rho\Gamma^{\mu\nu}\gamma_\rho
=(D-4)\Gamma^{\mu\nu}.
\]
Using
\[
\slashed r\,\slashed s
=(r\cdot s)+\Gamma^{\mu\nu}r_\mu s_\nu,
\]
the first same-bilinear chain becomes
\[
\boxed{
\gamma^\rho\slashed r_1P_R\slashed r_2\gamma_\rho
=
D(r_1\cdot r_2)P_R
+(D-4)\Gamma^{\mu\nu}r_{1\mu}r_{2\nu}P_R.
}
\]
The second same-bilinear chain has the corresponding \(P_L\) identity. Thus these graphs generate only \((0,0)\) and \((2,0)\), or \((0,0)\) and \((0,2)\), respectively.

For \(D=4-2\epsilon\),
\[
D-4=-2\epsilon.
\]
The same-bilinear rank-two numerator is retained through integration. P1.6a shows that its antisymmetric tensor integral is UV finite for nonexceptional off-shell external momenta; this term therefore does not multiply a UV pole or generate a UV-pole-induced finite local counterterm in these two three-propagator vertex graphs.

This is an exact numerator identity; the loop integrations of the two same-bilinear graphs have **not** been performed in P1.5c+.

## 5. Why SS+TS+ST+TT reconstructs the complete cross-source numerator

For a cross-source graph, the loop numerator has the form
\[
(q_A+s\ell)^2\,x_\alpha y_\beta\,
\mathcal D_i^{\alpha\beta},
\]
where \(\mathcal D_i^{\alpha\beta}\) is exactly the four-term decomposition in Sections 2–3. After the degree-two UV Taylor expansion, the common loop tensor is \(I_{\alpha\beta}^{(i)}\). Its four contractions are:

- SS: \(g^{\alpha\beta}I_{\alpha\beta}^{(i)}\), the full D-dimensional trace;
- TS: contraction of \(I_{\alpha\beta}^{(i)}\) with the left antisymmetric gamma structure;
- ST: contraction with the right antisymmetric gamma structure;
- TT: the unprojected \(I_{\alpha\beta}^{(i)}\) contracted with the two rank-two strings.

The SS trace and both antisymmetric projections were independently checked against P1.4b0 and P1.4b1. The full TT tensor was evaluated in P1.4b2 and combined in P1.5b. Consequently there is no fifth Dirac-rank sector left over in these four integrated cross-source graphs.

The color-weighted TT tensor \(J_{\alpha\beta}\) is symmetric. Its metric contraction is the direct opposite-chirality tensor seed, which is evanescent in four dimensions. The momentum-projected components of \(J_{\alpha\beta}\) must not be collapsed into that metric trace: the complete \(J_{\alpha\beta}X^{\alpha\beta}\) remains a D-dimensional tensor-derivative structure until a declared operator-basis projection is performed.

## 6. Closure statement and strict limitations

**Established:**
1. exact arbitrary-D Dirac-rank closure of the four cross-source chains on SS, TS, ST and TT;
2. exact arbitrary-D rank-zero/rank-two reduction of the two same-bilinear chains;
3. reproduction of all P1.5a relative signs from the direct Clifford decompositions;
4. no rank-three or rank-four gamma string is generated by the six listed one-gluon Feynman-gauge four-fermion exchange numerators in the original direct pairing;
5. the \(D-4=-2\epsilon\) same-bilinear tensor numerator is identified; P1.6a establishes that its associated off-shell tensor integral is UV finite.

**Not established:**
1. the UV poles of the two same-bilinear exchange graphs;
2. the external-quark wavefunction counterterms and complete six-graph QCD pole;
3. external-background-gluon Green functions and Ward identities;
4. closure of the full gauge-covariant dimension-eight operator basis, including \(4\psi F_{\mu\nu}\), gauge-EOM and EOM operators;
5. the physical/evanescent/EOM \(Z\) block, anomalous dimension, or finite UV-minus-EFT Wilson coefficient.

The rank-three/rank-four evanescent seeds retained in the wider source-pair Green-basis audit are not deleted. They are simply not generated by the six specific direct-pairing one-gluon QCD numerator chains audited here. Other source sectors, crossed pairings, and background-field amplitudes must be tested independently.
