## 1. Physical weak generators and Yukawa endpoints

Use the physical field generators
\[
R_{\bar L}^a=-t^{aT},\qquad
R_H^a=+t^a,\qquad
R_{H^\dagger}^a=-t^{aT},\qquad
R_L^a=+t^a.
\]
The two Yukawa tensors are singlets:
\[
\boxed{
(R_{\bar L}^a+R_H^a)T_0=0,
\qquad
(R_{H^\dagger}^a+R_L^a)T_0=0.
}
\]
The heavy \(E\) is an \(SU(2)_L\) singlet and contributes no generator. Thus the local gauge variation at each Yukawa vertex cancels between its two light fields. The external-leg/LSZ terms remain part of the full Green-function Ward identity and are not removed by this vertex-level singlet identity.

The two singlet identities are checked for all three generators and all 16 weak-index components (96/96).

## 2. Fermion-line endpoint terms

The A and B sides have opposite physical propagator momenta. With
\[
r_A^W=r_A^Y+q,\qquad r_B^Y=r_B^W+q,
\]
the exact off-shell contractions are
\[
\boxed{
S_F(-r_A^W)\slashed qP_LS_F(-r_A^Y)
=S_F(-r_A^W)P_R-P_LS_F(-r_A^Y),
}
\]
\[
\boxed{
S_F(r_B^Y)\slashed qP_LS_F(r_B^W)
=P_LS_F(r_B^W)-S_F(r_B^Y)P_R.
}
\]
Each expression has two endpoint terms:
- the term adjacent to the internal-W endpoint;
- the term adjacent to the Yukawa vertex.

The A/B signs and propagator orientations must be retained when multiplying by the ordered weak generators. The identities are checked as 4-by-4 matrix identities (32/32 Dirac components across both orientations).

## 3. Scalar-line and seagull endpoint terms

For \(\Delta(r)=1/r^2\), the scalar-line contractions are
\[
\boxed{
\Delta(r_A^W)\,
q\cdot(r_A^W+r_A^Y)\,
\Delta(r_A^Y)
=\Delta(r_A^Y)-\Delta(r_A^W),
}
\]
\[
\boxed{
\Delta(r_B^Y)\,
q\cdot(r_B^W+r_B^Y)\,
\Delta(r_B^W)
=\Delta(r_B^W)-\Delta(r_B^Y).
}
\]
The scalar seagull is
\[
V_{\rm sg}^{ab,\alpha\mu}
=ig_2^2\{t^a,t^b\}g^{\alpha\mu},
\qquad
q_\alpha V_{\rm sg}^{ab,\alpha\mu}
=ig_2^2\{t^a,t^b\}q^\mu.
\]
Its contraction is nonzero. The scalar-line propagator difference and this \(q^\mu\) contact term must be combined before assigning the complete scalar endpoint contribution.

The two oriented scalar identities are checked exactly (2/2). The seagull group tensor is separately checked in the physical-representation bridge; the Lorentz-weighted scalar-line/seagull sum remains open.

## 4. Internal-W endpoint and three-W insertion

For all incoming momenta \((q,k,-k-q)\),
\[
q^\alpha\Gamma_{\alpha\mu\nu}
=\mathcal K_{\mu\nu}(k+q)-\mathcal K_{\mu\nu}(k),
\qquad
\mathcal K_{\mu\nu}(k)=k^2g_{\mu\nu}-k_\mu k_\nu.
\]
With Feynman-gauge propagators,
\[
D_F(k+q)[\mathcal K(k+q)-\mathcal K(k)]D_F(k)
=D_F(k)-D_F(k+q)+R_{\rm FG},
\]
\[
R_{{\rm FG},\mu\nu}
=\frac{k_\mu k_\nu-(k+q)_\mu(k+q)_\nu}
{k^2(k+q)^2}.
\]
The first two terms are the propagator-difference pieces that must be matched to the internal-W endpoint terms generated by the A/B matter-line WTIs. The nonzero \(R_{\rm FG}\) is retained as a separate gauge-fixing/Slavnov–Taylor remainder.

The bare three-W contraction and the Feynman-gauge chain decomposition are checked for all 16 Lorentz components each.

## 5. Full endpoint ledger

The longitudinal contraction of the complete five-point Green function must organize into
\[
q_\alpha\mathcal M_5^{a\alpha}
=
\mathcal B_{\rm Yukawa}^a
+\mathcal B_{\rm intW}^a
+\mathcal B_{\rm scalar}^a
+\mathcal R_{\rm FG}^a
+\mathcal B_{\rm ext/LSZ}^a
+\mathcal B_{\rm CT/gh}^a.
\]
Here:
- \(\mathcal B_{\rm Yukawa}^a\) cancels at each gauge-singlet Yukawa vertex after both light-field transformations are included;
- \(\mathcal B_{\rm intW}^a\) is the sum of the A/B matter-line endpoint terms and the three-W propagator-difference terms;
- \(\mathcal B_{\rm scalar}^a\) combines ordinary scalar-line insertions with the seagulls;
- \(\mathcal R_{\rm FG}^a\) is the nonzero Feynman-gauge remainder;
- \(\mathcal B_{\rm ext/LSZ}^a\) contains the four external-leg emissions and the shifted four-point Ward variation;
- \(\mathcal B_{\rm CT/gh}^a\) contains field, parameter and operator counterterms together with the gauge-fixing/ghost Slavnov–Taylor completion.

This is the target decomposition, not a claim that every term has already canceled. In the unbroken \(SU(2)_L\) phase there are no electroweak Goldstone modes for these generators, but Faddeev–Popov ghosts remain. A broken-phase extension must restore Goldstone vertices and fermion mass terms together.
