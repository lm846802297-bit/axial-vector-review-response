# RC-001 Higgs-source and fermion-EOM map for the original axial model

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Workstream:** PR #11, QLD8 source/EOM reduction  
**Scope:** model-specific expansion of the Higgs EOM source and the leading fermion EOMs before Fierz/basis reduction. This is a source and operator census, not a completed fermionic dimension-eight basis.

## 1. Why the flavor assumption must be stated separately from gauge invariance

The original axial model uses
\[
x(L_e)=x(e_R)=0,\quad
x(L_\mu)=+1,\quad x(\mu_R)=-1,\quad
x(L_\tau)=-1,\quad x(\tau_R)=+1,\quad x(H)=0.
\]
For a renormalizable charged-lepton Yukawa \(\bar L_i H e_{Rj}\), the \(U(1)_X\) charge is
\[
q_X(\bar L_i H e_{Rj})=-x(L_i)+x(e_{Rj}).
\]
The exact charge matrix, with rows \(L_e,L_\mu,L_\tau\) and columns \(e_R,\mu_R,\tau_R\), is
\[
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-1&+1\\
-1&-2&0\\
+1&0&+2
\end{pmatrix}.}
\]
Therefore:
- the diagonal \(\mu\) and \(\tau\) renormalizable Yukawas are gauge-forbidden;
- the electron diagonal Yukawa is allowed;
- the off-diagonal \(\bar L_\mu H\tau_R\) and \(\bar L_\tau H\mu_R\) Yukawas are also gauge-allowed.

The last two entries are set to zero in the **original flavor-diagonal model assumption**, as shown by its block-diagonal \(\mu/\tau\) mass matrix and its displayed UV Lagrangian. They must not be described as \(U(1)_X\)-forbidden. If the flavor-diagonal assumption is relaxed, those two allowed entries enlarge the charged-lepton mass matrix and the Higgs source. This note keeps the original model unchanged and sets them to zero.

## 2. Explicit leading-renormalizable Higgs source

Use the project convention
\[
D^2H=-f_HH-\mathcal J_H,\qquad
f_H=-\mu_H^2+2\lambda_H(H^\dagger H)+\lambda_{HS}(S^\dagger S).
\]
For the SM quark sector and the original flavor-diagonal charged-lepton sector,
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
(\mathcal J_H^{(4)})^k={}&
\bar d_RY_d^\dagger Q_L^k
+\epsilon_{ik}\bar Q_L^iY_u u_R
+y_e^*\bar e_R L_e^k.
\end{aligned}}
\]
The quark Yukawa matrices are the full three-generation matrices. The charged-lepton renormalizable source contains only the electron entry in the original model. The \(\bar\tau_RL_\mu\) and \(\bar\mu_RL_\tau\) source terms are gauge-allowed but vanish by the stated flavor-diagonal assumption; the \(\bar\mu_RL_\mu\) and \(\bar\tau_RL_\tau\) terms are forbidden at dimension four.

### 2.1 Dimension-five axial source

With
\[
\mathcal L_{\rm EFT}^{(5)}
=-C_{5,\mu}S\bar L_\mu H\mu_R
-C_{5,\tau}S^*\bar L_\tau H\tau_R+\mathrm{h.c.},
\]
the hermitian-conjugate terms contribute to the \(H^\dagger\) variation. The dimension-five source is
\[
\boxed{
(\mathcal J_H^{(5)})^k
=C_{5,\mu}^*S^*\bar\mu_R L_\mu^k
+C_{5,\tau}^*S\bar\tau_R L_\tau^k.
}
\]
Each term is \(U(1)_X\)-neutral:
\[
x(S^*)+x(\bar\mu_R)+x(L_\mu)=-2+1+1=0,
\]
\[
x(S)+x(\bar\tau_R)+x(L_\tau)=+2-1-1=0.
\]
The coefficient follows the RC-001 Schur-complement convention \(C_{5,\ell}=-y_\ell\lambda_\ell/M_{E_\ell}\).

The dimension-five source is \(O(M_E^{-1})\). When it appears in the \(O(M_E^{-4})\) source operators below, its cross terms start at \(O(M_E^{-5})\), and its source-square starts at \(O(M_E^{-6})\). It is therefore outside the strict \(O(M_E^{-4})\) truncation, not identically zero.

## 3. Product-rule expansion of the source derivatives

The full source entering the Higgs EOM is
\[
\mathcal J_H=\mathcal J_H^{(4)}+\mathcal J_H^{(5)}+\cdots.
\]
At the retained leading-renormalizable order,
\[
\begin{aligned}
D_\mu(\mathcal J_H^{(4)})^k={}&
(D_\mu\bar d_R)Y_d^\dagger Q_L^k
+\bar d_RY_d^\dagger D_\mu Q_L^k\\
&+\epsilon_{ik}\left[
(D_\mu\bar Q_L^i)Y_u u_R
+\bar Q_L^iY_uD_\mu u_R
\right]\\
&+(D_\mu\bar e_R)y_e^*L_e^k
+\bar e_Ry_e^*D_\mu L_e^k.
\end{aligned}
\]
The derivative is the covariant derivative in the Higgs-doublet representation on the complete source. The individual fermion derivatives carry their own representations; the product rule is not permission to replace them by ordinary derivatives or to drop gauge connections.

The operators generated by the Higgs-EOM reduction are retained as
\[
\mathcal O_{F\mathcal J_H}^{(8),A}
=\frac i2F_A^{\mu\nu}\left[
(D_\mu\mathcal J_H)^\dagger t_A D_\nu H
+(D_\mu H)^\dagger t_A D_\nu\mathcal J_H
\right],
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal O_{S,\mathrm{src}}\doteq{}&
(\partial_\mu f_H)\left[
H^\dagger D^\mu\mathcal J_H+\mathrm{h.c.}
\right]\\
&+f_H\left[
(D_\mu H)^\dagger D^\mu\mathcal J_H+\mathrm{h.c.}
\right]
+(D_\mu\mathcal J_H)^\dagger D^\mu\mathcal J_H.
\end{aligned}
\]
For strict \(O(M_E^{-4})\), insert \(\mathcal J_H^{(4)}\) in these expressions. The \(\mathcal J_H^{(5)}\) terms are recorded as higher order.

## 4. Leading fermion EOMs and their actual reduction power

For the renormalizable SM quark/electron sector, the leading equations are
\[
\begin{aligned}
i\slashed D Q_L&=Y_u\widetilde H u_R+Y_dH d_R,\\
i\slashed D u_R&=Y_u^\dagger\widetilde H^\dagger Q_L,\\
i\slashed D d_R&=Y_d^\dagger H^\dagger Q_L,\\
i\slashed D L_e&=y_eH e_R,\\
i\slashed D e_R&=y_e^*H^\dagger L_e.
\end{aligned}
\]
For the light axial charged leptons, the first nonzero Yukawa source is dimension five:
\[
\begin{aligned}
i\slashed D L_\mu&=C_{5,\mu}S H\mu_R,
&
i\slashed D\mu_R&=C_{5,\mu}^*S^*H^\dagger L_\mu,\\
i\slashed D L_\tau&=C_{5,\tau}S^*H\tau_R,
&
i\slashed D\tau_R&=C_{5,\tau}^*S H^\dagger L_\tau.
\end{aligned}
\]
These \(C_5\) terms are higher order in the present \(O(M_E^{-4})\) source reduction, as stated in §2.1.

Crucially, the displayed source operators contain \(D_\mu\) on fermion bilinears with a **free Lorentz index** contracted with \(F_A^{\mu\nu}\) or another derivative. The Dirac EOM fixes only the gamma trace, not the complete vector derivative. In \(d\) dimensions one may write
\[
D_\mu\psi=\frac1d\gamma_\mu\slashed D\psi
+\left(D_\mu-\frac1d\gamma_\mu\slashed D\right)\psi,
\qquad
\gamma^\mu\left(D_\mu-\frac1d\gamma_\mu\slashed D\right)\psi=0.
\]
Only the first term is directly replaceable by the fermion EOM. The gamma-traceless derivative remainder is not zero and must be retained or reduced by explicit IBP/Fierz identities in a declared operator basis.

Thus neither \(\mathcal O_{F\mathcal J_H}^{(8)}\) nor \(\mathcal O_{S,\mathrm{src}}\) can be discarded merely by citing the fermion EOM. Their spinor, weak-isospin, color, and flavor contractions must be exposed before Fierz reduction. Evanescent structures must be tracked if the reduction is carried out in \(d=4-2\epsilon\).
