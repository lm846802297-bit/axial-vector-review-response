Scope limitation:

evaluate a loop integral, UV pole, Z matrix, anomalous dimension, finite
Wilson coefficient, or UV-EFT matching result. The candidate gamma-rank

it is not a proof that the one-loop evanescent basis is closed.

Additional interpretation:
The d-dimensional identity is Gamma^mu Gamma^nu P_R tensor P_L Gamma_nu Gamma_mu
= D P_R tensor P_L + sigma^{mu nu} P_R tensor sigma_{mu nu} P_L.
