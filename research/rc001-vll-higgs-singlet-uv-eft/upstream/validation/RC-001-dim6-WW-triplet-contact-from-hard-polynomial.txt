RC-001 Q_LD^(3) contact component from the nonexceptional WW hard polynomial

Hard polynomial momentum basis:
  C_hard^fin = a v_D + b v_minus + c v_plus
  v_D = p3+p4 = -(p1+p2)
  v_minus = p2-p3
  v_plus = p2+p3

Exact coefficients:
  a = -1/2 + L/2
  b = -1/32 + 7L/16
  c = 7/32 - 5L/16
  L=ln(M_E^2/mu_R^2).

Only v_D is identified with the matrix element of Q_LD^(3), whose
derivative acts on Hdag sigma^I L. The raw +slash(P) amplitude coefficient
is -a because v_D=-P. In the all-incoming Fourier convention, the operator
coefficient is therefore a.

At mu_R=M_E:
  C_Q3/C_LD = -g2^2/(32 pi^2),
  where C_LD=|y_l|^2/M_E^2 and the common loop normalization is 1/(16 pi^2).

The corresponding EFT one-W contact from the adjoint derivative is
  i g2 C_Q3 epsilon^{IJK}
    (barL sigma^I H) gamma^mu W_mu^J (Hdag sigma^K L).

The contact sign follows the coefficient of v_D, not the raw +slash(P)
amplitude label.

Boundary:
  This is only the v_D/Q_LD^(3) component of the local polynomial.
  The v_minus and v_plus structures are not yet assigned to operators.
  The UV one-loop external-W five-point form factor and complete
  Slavnov-Taylor sum are not calculated here.
