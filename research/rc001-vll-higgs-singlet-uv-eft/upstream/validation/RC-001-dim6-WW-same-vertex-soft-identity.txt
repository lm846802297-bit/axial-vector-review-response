RC-001 same-Yukawa-vertex pure-WW sub-block

External ordering:
  barL(p1), H(p2), Hdag(p3), L(p4), all incoming.
  P = p1+p2 is the heavy momentum at the left Yukawa vertex.
  Q = p3+p4 = -P is the heavy momentum at the conjugate right vertex.

Same-vertex loop routings:
  LD-W-03 (barL-H):
    W denominator: k^2
    light fermion denominator: (k+p1)^2
    light scalar denominator: (k-p2)^2
    stripped numerator: gamma_mu (slash(k+p1)) (2 p2-k)^mu
  LD-W-04 (L-Hdag):
    W denominator: k^2
    light fermion denominator: (k+p4)^2
    light scalar denominator: (k-p3)^2
    stripped numerator: (2 p3-k)_mu slash(k+p4) gamma^mu

Explicit 4D Euclidean matrix checks:
  PASS LD-W-03 leading large-k numerator = -k^2 * identity.
  PASS LD-W-04 leading large-k numerator = -k^2 * identity.
  Therefore each stripped triangle has a logarithmic UV pole with unit
  scalar residue and the displayed numerator sign, before the common
  Feynman-rule phase, gauge coupling, Yukawa factors and weak generator.
  The weak generator factor for each is -3/4 T0, from the exact SU(2)
  projection in research/RC-001-dim6-SU2-attachment-projection.md.

Chirality and tree matching:
  P_R (slash(P)+M_E) P_L = slash(P) P_L.
  The heavy mass numerator is killed by the chiral projectors.
  For |P^2| << M_E^2:
    P_R S_E(P) P_L -> -slash(P) P_L/M_E^2 + O(M_E^-4).
  The EFT derivative momentum is q_D=p3+p4=-P, so the tree Q_LD
  insertion carries the same -slash(P)/M_E^2 structure.

Matching consequence:
  The W-loop denominators are entirely light; the heavy propagator depends
  only on P and is independent of loop momentum k.
  The full-theory same-vertex graph at O(M_E^-2) is therefore the same
  massless triangle form factor multiplying the tree-level Q_LD insertion.
  The EFT insertion reproduces this soft graph exactly.
  In the hard region, the loop integrals contain no M_E-dependent
  denominator; after Taylor expansion they are scaleless in dimensional
  regularization. Their UV/IR pieces must be kept separate until matching,
  but their hard-minus-soft matching contribution is zero.

Conclusion:
  LD-W-03 and LD-W-04 are pure-soft at O(g2^2/M_E^2) and generate no
  finite hard Wilson coefficient in UV-minus-EFT matching. They remain
  necessary in the EFT soft sum, operator renormalization and full Ward
  identity. This does not close external-leg corrections, parameter/
  operator counterterms, the external-W five-point form factor, or the
  complete Slavnov-Taylor sum.

Reproduction:
  calculations/RC-001-dim6-WW-same-vertex-soft-identity.py checks the
  leading numerator, chiral heavy propagator projection and q_D=-P identity.
  This is a local algebraic factorization check, not diagram-package
  validation or a complete finite triangle integration.
