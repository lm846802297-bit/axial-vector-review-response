RC-001 six-pair internal-W hard-matching summary

External ordering:
  barL(p1), H(p2), Hdag(p3), L(p4), all incoming.
  p1+p2+p3+p4=0.

Cross-vertex classes LD-W-01/02/05/06:
  Nonexceptional d-dimensional hard/soft pole closure is recorded in
  research/RC-001-dim6-nonexceptional-matching-kinematics.md §§3.1-3.4.
  Their weak-triplet weighted hard pole residue is
    (1/2) p1 + (3/8) p2 + (3/4) p3.
  Their MS-bar finite local hard polynomial is
    (1/2-L/2) p1 +(11/16-3L/8) p2 +(1/4-3L/4) p3,
  with L=ln(M_E^2/mu_R^2).

Same-vertex classes LD-W-03/04:
  Their SU(2) generator tensor is -3/4 T0 for each class.
  Their heavy propagator carries only the external momentum P=p1+p2,
  independent of the W-loop momentum.
  The chiral heavy propagator reduces to -slash(P)/M_E^2.
  The EFT derivative momentum q_D=p3+p4=-P gives the same insertion.
  Their W-loop integrals are pure-soft and reproduced by the EFT;
  the hard-region loop integrals are scaleless.
  Therefore their hard-minus-EFT contribution at O(M_E^-2) is zero.
  Details: research/RC-001-dim6-WW-same-vertex-soft-matching.md.

Six-pair result:
  At the internal-W four-point hard-matching level, the only nonzero
  hard local term in this fixed ordering is the triplet cross-vertex
  polynomial above. The same-vertex singlet pairs contribute no hard
  threshold at this order.

At mu_R=M_E:
  C_hard = (1/2) p1 +(11/16) p2 +(1/4) p3.

Local reproduction:
  The symbolic six-pair assembly script reproduces the cross-box
  pole/finite polynomial and adds the zero same-vertex hard contribution.
  Source: calculations/RC-001-dim6-WW-six-pair-hard-summary.py.

Boundary:
  This is the internal-W exchange hard-matching sub-block for one fixed
  ordered tree exchange and unit multiplicity. It is not the full
  renormalized four-point amplitude or a complete Wilson coefficient.
  External legs, parameter/operator counterterms, external-W five-point
  form factor, full Slavnov-Taylor sum, and common EOM/IBP basis projection
  remain open. No diagram-package validation is claimed.
