RC-001 Abelian QLD8 IBP/EOM reduction — exact coefficient audit

Convention:
  D_mu = partial_mu + i g_A q_H A_mu
  [D_mu,D_nu]H = i g_A q_H F_mu_nu H
  X=H^dagger H, Y=S^dagger S
  j_H^mu = i H^dagger <->D^mu H
  f_H = -mu_H^2 + 2 lambda_H X + lambda_HS Y
  D^2 H = -f_H H - J_H

Exact Abelian IBP identity:
  O_Q = -i (D_mu H)^dagger D_nu H Box F^mu nu
  O_Q ~= -i F^mu nu Box[(D_mu H)^dagger D_nu H]
  Box[(D_mu H)^dagger D_nu H]
    = (D^2 D_mu H)^dagger D_nu H
      + 2(D_rho D_mu H)^dagger D^rho D_nu H
      + (D_mu H)^dagger D^2 D_nu H
  Therefore:
    O_P + O_Q/2 ~= O_E,H
  O_E,H = -i/2 F^mu nu[
      (D^2 D_mu H)^dagger D_nu H
      + (D_mu H)^dagger D^2 D_nu H ]

Its one-background vertex is:
  B_E,H = B_P + B_Q/2 = (P+P') T^mu/2
  This is transverse and vanishes for P=P'=0.

Commutator:
  [D^2,D_mu]H = i g_A q_H[
      (partial_rho F^rho_mu)H + 2 F^rho_mu D_rho H ]

Writing O_E,H = O_E,H(ordered) + O_comm gives:
  O_E,H(ordered) =
    -i/2 F^mu nu[
      (D_mu D^2 H)^dagger D_nu H
      + (D_mu H)^dagger D_nu D^2 H ]
  O_comm =
    -(g_A q_H/2) F^mu nu (partial_rho F^rho_mu) partial_nu X
    -g_A q_H F^mu nu F^rho_mu[
       (D_rho H)^dagger D_nu H
       +(D_nu H)^dagger D_rho H ]

Higgs EOM reduction:
  O_E,H(ordered) ~= f_H O_FH^(6)
    - (1/2) F^mu nu j_H_mu partial_nu f_H
    + O_FJ_H^(8) + O_EOM
  O_FH^(6) = i F^mu nu (D_mu H)^dagger D_nu H
  O_FJ_H^(8) = i/2 F^mu nu[
      (D_mu J_H)^\dagger D_nu H
      +(D_mu H)^\dagger D_nu J_H ]
  O_EOM is proportional to D^2 H + f_H H + J_H.

Potential/current companions:
  f_H O_FH^(6)
    = [-mu_H^2 + 2 lambda_H X + lambda_HS Y] O_FH^(6)
  -(1/2) F j_H partial f_H
    = -lambda_H F^mu nu j_H_mu partial_nu X
      -(lambda_HS/2) F^mu nu j_H_mu partial_nu Y

Gauge-EOM alternative (Abelian, with -F^2/4 and the stated D convention):
  partial_rho F^rho_mu = g_A J_mu^tot
  O_comm,gauge ~= -(g_A^2 q_H/2)
       F^mu nu J_mu^tot partial_nu X
  Keep either partial.F or the total-current representative, never both.
