# Major Point 1 — \(R_\xi\) gauge-fixing and quadratic-sector derivation (A1)

**Status:** gauge-fixing functions and cancellation of gauge--Goldstone quadratic mixing are fixed. The full ghost interaction expansion and the complete two-loop-order self-energy set are not yet claimed.

## 1. Scalar and gauge conventions

Take
\[
H=\begin{pmatrix}G^+\\(v+h+iG^0)/\sqrt2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad S=(v_s+s+ia)/\sqrt2,
\]
with
\[
m_W=\frac{g_2v}{2},\quad
m_Z=\frac{\sqrt{g_2^2+g_Y^2}\,v}{2},\quad
m_X=m_{A'}=2g_Xv_s.
\]
The neutral SM fields are \(Z_\mu=c_W W_\mu^3-s_W B_\mu\) and \(A_\mu=s_W W_\mu^3+c_WB_\mu\). The singlet \(S\) is neutral under the SM group and \(H\) is neutral under \(U(1)_X\). This note uses a diagonal gauge-kinetic basis at the reference scale; any radiatively generated hypercharge--\(X\) kinetic mixing is a separate insertion and must be included consistently if retained.

Choose \(\delta A_\mu^A=+\partial_\mu\alpha^A+\cdots\) and \(\delta\Phi=-i\alpha^Ag_AT^A\Phi\). Normalize the broken-generator parameters so that
\[
\delta G^\pm=-m_W\alpha^\pm+\cdots,\quad
\delta G^0=-m_Z\alpha_Z+\cdots,\quad
\delta a=-m_X\alpha_X+\cdots.
\]
For the nonlinear radial terms, \(\delta G_W^a=-m_W(1+h/v)\alpha_W^a+\cdots\), \(\delta G^0=-m_Z(1+h/v)\alpha_Z+\cdots\), and \(\delta a=-m_X(1+s/v_S)\alpha_X\).

## 2. Linear \(R_\xi\) gauge-fixing functions

Choose
\[
\begin{aligned}
F^\pm&=\partial^\mu W_\mu^\pm\mp i\xi_Wm_WG^\pm,\\
F^Z&=\partial^\mu Z_\mu-\xi_Zm_ZG^0,\\
F^A&=\partial^\mu A_\mu,\\
F^X&=\partial^\mu A'_\mu-\xi_Xm_Xa.
\end{aligned}
\]
The gauge-fixing Lagrangian is
\[
\mathcal L_{\rm GF}
=-\frac{1}{\xi_W}F^+F^-
-\frac{1}{2\xi_Z}(F^Z)^2
-\frac{1}{2\xi_A}(F^A)^2
-\frac{1}{2\xi_X}(F^X)^2.
\]
The corresponding gauge-boson propagators have longitudinal denominators \(k^2-\xi_i m_i^2\), and the Goldstone masses are
\[
m_{G^\pm}^2=\xi_Wm_W^2,\quad
m_{G^0}^2=\xi_Zm_Z^2,\quad
m_a^2=\xi_Xm_X^2.
\]

## 3. Quadratic mixing cancellation

With the covariant derivative convention \(D_\mu=\partial_\mu+ig_AT^AA_\mu^A\), the scalar kinetic terms contain the bilinear gauge--Goldstone mixings with the sign opposite to the cross terms from \(\mathcal L_{\rm GF}\). Integrating by parts, the cross terms generated by the displayed \(\mathcal L_{\rm GF}\) cancel these bilinears exactly. Thus the quadratic propagators are diagonal in the gauge/Goldstone sectors.

The CP-even tree mass matrix remains
\[
{\cal M}_{hs,0}^2=
\begin{pmatrix}
2\lambda_Hv^2&\lambda_{HS}vv_s\\
\lambda_{HS}vv_s&2\lambda_Sv_s^2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
For the reference matching point \(\lambda_{HS}^{\rm EFT}(M_E)=0\), the renormalized low-energy tree off-diagonal entry is set to zero at that matching scale; the UV portal parameter is chosen to compensate the finite heavy threshold. This is a boundary condition, not a symmetry.

## 4. Faddeev--Popov ghost action

Define collectively \(\alpha^A=(\alpha^\pm,\alpha_Z,\alpha_A,\alpha_X)\), and the gauge-fixing map \(F^A\) above. The ghost action is fixed without ambiguity by
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{\rm FP}
=-\bar c^A
\frac{\delta F^A[\Phi^\alpha]}{\delta\alpha^B}
c^B\bigg|_{\alpha=0},
}
\]
where \(\Phi^\alpha\) denotes the gauge-transformed gauge and scalar fields. This functional derivative must be evaluated using the full nonlinear \(SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\times U(1)_X\) transformations; retaining only the constant Goldstone shifts gives the ghost mass terms but misses the \(h,s,G,a\)-dependent ghost vertices.

The quadratic ghost masses are
\[
m_{c_W}^2=\xi_Wm_W^2,\quad
m_{c_Z}^2=\xi_Zm_Z^2,\quad
m_{c_X}^2=\xi_Xm_X^2,
\]
with the photon ghost massless. The non-Abelian charged/neutral electroweak ghost interactions are those generated by the standard SM gauge transformations. The \(U(1)_X\) ghost has no non-Abelian self-interaction. Its scalar-dependent FP operator is \(\xi_Xm_X^2(1+s/v_S)\), so
\[
\mathcal L_{\bar c_Xc_Xs}=-\xi_X\frac{m_X^2}{v_S}s\,\bar c_Xc_X
=-2\xi_Xm_Xg_Xs\,\bar c_Xc_X,
\]
where \(m_X=2g_Xv_S\). This corrects the factor-of-two-low coefficient in the first A1 draft. There is no direct \(h\bar c_Xc_X\) vertex in the gauge basis because \(H\) is \(U(1)_X\)-neutral.

The electroweak ghost vertices involving \(h,G^\pm,G^0\), and the mixed electroweak ghost vertices are to be generated from the same functional derivative. They must be retained in the \(O(\hbar^2)\) gauge-complete two-point calculation wherever they contribute.

## 5. Immediate checks and boundary

The following identities are fixed by construction:
- the \(W^\pm-G^\mp\), \(Z-G^0\), and \(A'-a\) bilinear mixings cancel;
- the Goldstone and ghost masses are proportional to the corresponding \(\xi_i m_i^2\);
- no gauge-basis \(hA'A'\), \(sWW\), or \(sZZ\) vertex exists at zero portal;
- portal-induced vertices are \(O(\hbar)\) in the chosen conditional matching counting.
