# Major Point 1 — perturbative-order closure and gauge-sector entry map (2026-11-10)

**Purpose.** Turn the broad UV diagram census into an order-by-order calculation plan. This note deliberately does not add a selected gauge-dependent subset to the existing fermion result and does not claim a physical pole calculation.

## 1. Model and power counting

Use the original one-VEV model and the scalar potential
\[
V=-\mu_H^2H^\dagger H-\mu_S^2S^\dagger S+
\lambda_H(H^\dagger H)^2+\lambda_S(S^\dagger S)^2+
\lambda_{HS}(H^\dagger H)(S^\dagger S).
\]
The retained reference matching condition is
\[
\lambda_{HS}^{\rm EFT}(M_E)=0,\qquad
\lambda_{HS}^{\rm UV}(M_E)=-\Delta\lambda_{HS}^{E}.
\]
Because \(\Delta\lambda_{HS}^{E}\) is a one-heavy-fermion-loop threshold, this reference choice assigns
\[
\lambda_{HS}^{\rm UV}=O(\hbar).
\]
It is a conditional cancellation benchmark; the renormalized portal remains an independent model parameter away from this reference point.

The distinction between loop order and coupling order is essential. A graph with one portal vertex and one ordinary scalar/gauge vertex is still one loop topologically, but is \(O(\hbar^2)\) in this combined counting because the portal itself is \(O(\hbar)\).

## 2. Leading \(O(\hbar)\) two-point matrix

At this order the nonzero new-physics contributions are the exact \(\mu,\tau+\mathrm{VLL}\) fermion determinant/self-energy matrix:
\[
\Pi^{(1)}_{ab}(p^2),\qquad a,b\in\{h,s\}.
\]
All four internal mass-eigenstate pairings are retained. The existing exact SVD calculation supplies the fermionic \(hh,hs,ss\) momentum-dependent entries and their absorptive light-lepton cuts.

The leading hard portal threshold is a local zero-momentum term and must be combined with the independently specified renormalized UV portal before interpreting \(\Pi_{hs}\). It is not an additional diagram to be added twice to the same full-theory amplitude.

The SM-only one-loop \(hh\) self-energy (top, \(W,Z\), Higgs/Goldstones, etc.) can be absorbed into a physical SM Higgs pole input only if the calculation explicitly defines that input and consistently subtracts the corresponding SM contribution. It cannot be included again on top of the measured SM pole mass/width. There is no gauge-basis tree-level \(sWW\), \(sZZ\), or \(hA'A'\) vertex when \(\lambda_{HS}=0\).

## 3. Portal-induced \(O(\hbar^2)\) completion

At the next order, \(\lambda_{HS}=O(\hbar)\) allows the following one-loop topologies. Their vertices and counterterm insertions must be generated from the same gauge-fixed Lagrangian.

| External entry | Internal sector/topology | Portal-dependent vertex content | Required companion terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| \(hs\) | CP-even scalar bubble | \(hhs\) with \(hhh\), or \(hss\) with \(sss\), and the corresponding allowed internal assignments | scalar tadpoles, mass/field counterterms |
| \(hs\) | Electroweak Goldstone bubble | \(hG_HG_H\) with \(sG_HG_H\) | \(R_\xi\) Goldstone masses, tadpoles, gauge-sector completion |
| \(hs\) | \(U(1)_X\) Goldstone bubble | \(haa\) with \(saa\) | \(A'\)-Goldstone mixing fixing, \(U(1)_X\) ghost and tadpole terms |
| \(hs\) | \(W,Z\) vector bubble | SM \(hVV\) with portal/mixing-induced \(sVV\) | longitudinal modes, Goldstones, ghosts, counterterms |
| \(hs\) | \(A'\) vector bubble | portal/mixing-induced \(hA'A'\) with \(sA'A'\) | \(U(1)_X\) Goldstone, ghost, tadpole, counterterms |
| \(hh,ss\) | Scalar/vector/Goldstone/ghost self-energies | all vertices generated by the same potential and covariant derivatives | diagonal mass and field counterterms |
| \(hh,hs,ss\) | One-loop tadpole insertions and parameter counterterms | \(\delta v,\delta v_s,\delta\lambda_i,\delta Z_{ij}\) | fixed tadpole prescription and vacuum conditions |

The \(hs\) scalar/Goldstone/vector bubbles in this table are one-loop topologies but next order in the combined \(\hbar\) counting. They must not be added to the leading fermion one-loop result and labelled as the same-order \(O(\hbar)\) correction.

## 4. Gauge-fixing and ghost completion

For an \(R_\xi\) calculation, the charged electroweak and \(U(1)_X\) Goldstone masses depend on \(\xi_W,\xi_Z,\xi_X\). The ghost Lagrangian must be derived from the same gauge-fixing functions. A gauge-parameter check must be performed on the renormalized pole positions (and, where appropriate, properly defined S-matrix residues), not on an isolated Goldstone or vector graph.

A useful staged check is:
1. derive the complete \(R_\xi\) quadratic and interaction Lagrangian after shifting \(H,S\);
2. extract all \(h,s,G_H,a,W,Z,A'\) vertices and ghost couplings;
3. generate all 1PI two-point, tadpole, and counterterm graphs for \(hh,hs,ss\);
4. reduce each graph to scalar PV functions with a common \(D=4-2\epsilon\) convention;
5. verify UV-pole cancellation and \(\xi\)-independence after combining the full set.

No \(\xi\)-dependent partial sum is to be used as a physical mixing prediction.

## 5. Double-counting ledger for the existing calculations

- The exact fermion \(hh,hs,ss\) matrix includes explicit \(\mu,\tau\) absorptive cuts. Do not retain a full externally inserted SM Higgs width and add the same \(\mu,\tau\) channels again.
- The \(s\to\mu\mu,\tau\tau\) widths from the same fermion sector must not be added twice.
- The heavy VLL portal threshold is already included in the chosen matching boundary. Do not add it again as a local \(hs\) mass insertion after imposing \(\lambda_{HS}^{\rm EFT}(M_E)=0\).
- The EFT light bubble and its dimension-five contact/tadpole term are already present in the archived Plan-C soft subset. A new EFT calculation must first compare diagram-by-diagram with that subset.
- The archived CP-even P2-lite bubble is a selected \(O(\hbar^2)\) contribution, not the complete next-order scalar/gauge answer.

## 6. Immediate calculation sequence and acceptance tests

**A1 — gauge-fixed Lagrangian:** write the \(R_\xi\) gauge-fixing and ghost Lagrangian for \(SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\times U(1)_X\), including the scalar shifts and Goldstone definitions. Check the quadratic mixing cancellation.

**A2 — vertex ledger:** machine-generate the complete \(h,s\) couplings to CP-even scalars, electroweak Goldstones, \(U(1)_X\) Goldstone, \(W,Z,A'\), and ghosts. Cross-check selected vertices by differentiating the gauge-fixed action.

**A3 — \(O(\hbar^2)\) \(hs\) diagrams:** calculate the portal-induced scalar, Goldstone, vector, and ghost contributions in one common \(R_\xi\) gauge. Keep the \(\xi\)-dependent pieces separate until the complete sum is assembled.

**A4 — diagonal entries:** calculate the corresponding \(hh,ss\) corrections and counterterms at the same order, not only \(hs\).

**A5 — renormalization closure:** impose the chosen tadpole/VEV prescription, include all parameter and field counterterms, and demonstrate cancellation of UV poles and matching-scale dependence.

**A6 — gauge check:** repeat the complete renormalized pole calculation at at least two \(\xi\) choices. The pole positions must agree up to the expected uncomputed higher order.

The calculation is accepted as a complete \(O(\hbar^2)\) correction only after A1–A6. Until then the exact fermion matrix remains a validated \(O(\hbar)\) sector result and the portal-induced scalar/gauge terms remain a next-order programme.

## 7. Scope lock

This workstream is Higgs–radial-singlet mixing. No dedicated \(h\to A'A'\) search recast is part of the present stage. Exotic decays may enter later only as physical observables computed from the completed propagator and vertices; they do not replace the self-energy/matching calculation.
