## 3. Erratum to the fifty-ninth review: muon-density convention

The fifty-ninth review described the plotted Caputo muon density as a net-muon quantity. That statement was too strong: the available public figure and repository evidence do not establish whether the plotted \(n_\mu\) denotes \(n_{\mu^-}\) or \(n_{\mu^-}-n_{\mu^+}\).

The current \`BOLLIG_MUON_DENSITY_AND_B1_SOURCE_AUDIT\` output reports both comparisons:
- \(n_{\mu^-}\) versus the Caputo plotted curve over the 12--15 km digitized vertices: mean absolute relative difference 0.472%, maximum 0.538%;
- \(n_{\mu^-}-n_{\mu^+}\) versus the plotted curve over 5--15 km: mean absolute relative difference 0.990%, maximum 1.863%.

The latter interval includes inner radii where the positive-muon fraction is larger. In the outer 12--15 km shell, the positive-muon fraction is already below about 0.086% at 12 km and is negligible by 15 km. The exact same-vertex 12--15 km net-density residual should be reported if the plotted convention is later established; the present evidence does not justify asserting that the plotted curve is definitely net density.

This correction does not change the B.17 source convention: the published B.17 susceptibility is the single negative-muon distribution with two spin states. Keep the source-density convention and the plotted-profile convention as separate questions.
