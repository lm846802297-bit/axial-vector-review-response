## Cross-document scientific conclusions

1. **B.1 energy variable:** the source integral uses the local production-point energy with threshold \(\omega_{\min}=m_V/N(r)\). The relation \(E_\infty=N(r)\omega_{\rm loc}\) is a variable transformation, not an unresolved physical ambiguity in B.1.
2. **B.2 energy/path convention:** the literal printed B.2 integrand holds \(\omega\) fixed along the Euclidean chord. The path-redshift-only variant is a separate diagnostic and is not full GR ray propagation.
3. **Operational Compton baseline:** B.14–B.16 remains the adopted reference approximation for continuity with the printed reduced equations. B.13 is the exact moving-muon alternative and must remain separate. The paper does not establish which private numerical implementation generated Fig. 7.
4. **Numerical closure is not physical validation:** independent B.1+B.2 and V8 cross-runtime checks close against the archived B.16 calculation, but the reconstructed profile is not the original Garching simulation table.
5. **Keep snapshots separate:** the independent B.1+B.2 report's strong-end residual (+92.0542%) and the later consolidated acceptance snapshot (+92.2600%) come from distinct calculation snapshots/configurations. Do not average them or treat them as one output.
6. **No empirical repair:** no source, opacity, coupling, luminosity, \(R_{\max}\), or axial-vector curve is to be adjusted to force agreement with Fig. 7.
