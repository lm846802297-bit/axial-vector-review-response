Interpretation:
- Zero UV portal and zero matched EFT portal are different choices.
- The reference condition lambda_HS^EFT(M_E)=0 requires a nonzero UV portal to cancel the finite VLL threshold. This is a stated benchmark choice, not a symmetry prediction.
- With the matched benchmark, the open A'A' channel makes the singlet-like pole broad and keeps the Higgs-like pole close to the SM width in this approximation.
- With A'A' closed, the exact-degenerate poles show width redistribution and a non-negligible complex mixing diagnostic. A dedicated two-pole lineshape/signal-strength fit is required before classifying this narrow region as allowed or excluded.
- The near-degenerate region remains NOT ASSESSED by a model-specific experimental recast.
