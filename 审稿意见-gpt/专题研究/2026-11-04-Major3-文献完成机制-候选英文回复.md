Realistic neutrino masses and mixing can be accommodated by supplementing the axial construction with the singlet-scalar type-I seesaw mechanism used in gauged U(1)_{L_mu-L_tau} models [1,3,4]. The relevant observation is that our lepton doublets have the same charges, Q_X(L_e,L_mu,L_tau)=(0,+1,-1), as in the conventional model, and H is neutral. The different charges of the right-handed charged leptons do not enter the neutrino mass operators.

An explicit completion introduces three SM-singlet right-handed neutrinos with Q_X(N_Re,N_Rmu,N_Rtau)=(0,+1,-1) and an additional SM-singlet scalar sigma with Q_X(sigma)=+1. Together with the existing Q_X(S)=+2 scalar, this realizes the two-singlet neutrino mass construction of Refs. [3,4]. The charge-one scalar generates the e-mu and e-tau Majorana entries, while S generates the mu-mu and tau-tau entries. With <H^0>=v_h/sqrt(2), <sigma>=v_sigma/sqrt(2), and <S>=v_s/sqrt(2), the mass matrices are

\[
m_D=\frac{v_h}{\sqrt2}\operatorname{diag}(y_e,y_\mu,y_\tau),\qquad
M_R=\begin{pmatrix}
M_{ee}&h_{e\mu}v_\sigma/\sqrt2&h_{e\tau}v_\sigma/\sqrt2\\
h_{e\mu}v_\sigma/\sqrt2&h_{\mu\mu}v_s/\sqrt2&M_{\mu\tau}\\
h_{e\tau}v_\sigma/\sqrt2&M_{\mu\tau}&h_{\tau\tau}v_s/\sqrt2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

In the seesaw regime, m_nu=-m_D M_R^{-1}m_D^T. All six independent entries of M_R are allowed, so the light-neutrino mass matrix is not forced to be block diagonal or aligned with the charged-lepton mass matrix. Indeed, for any nonsingular target m_nu and nonsingular diagonal m_D, the choice M_R=-m_D^T m_nu^{-1}m_D has precisely the allowed form. Thus the construction can reproduce a nontrivial PMNS matrix and the observed mass-squared differences. The added singlet fermions contribute zero net cubic and mixed gravitational U(1)_X anomalies.

The additional symmetry breaking is essential: with only the charge-two VEV, a residual Z_2 separates the electron family from the muon and tau families, and adding N_R alone does not remove this restriction. A nonzero <sigma> breaks that residual symmetry. After U(1)_X breaking and integrating out the heavy neutrinos, the low-energy theory contains the usual Weinberg operator and light Majorana neutrinos. Above the symmetry-breaking scale, the corresponding operators must retain the charged scalar fields required for gauge invariance. The mechanism is therefore the established L_mu-L_tau seesaw completion, with its field-content requirements made explicit.
